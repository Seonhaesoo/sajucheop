Sajucheop library · Spring rain that seeps in without a sound

The one who sees everything and says nothing.

Who you are

You seep into the low places and end up soaking everything. Your observation and empathy run deep; you catch the smallest shift in a person. Never loud, always certain — in the end it is dripping water that hollows the rock.

Strengths

  • Quiet observation that goes straight to the core.
  • Deep trust earned from a low, unthreatening posture.

Watch out for

  • You think so much that starting comes late.
  • You digest things alone until the cup overflows.

In love

You arrive like drizzle — nobody remembers when you got close. Intuition tells you what a partner feels before they do, which is a gift and a burden. You water everyone; make sure someone is raining on you.

At work

Depth work: research, medicine, psychology, writing, analysis, anything where noticing is the job. Quiet rooms and long timelines suit you; open-plan chaos does not.

Chemistry — who you combine and clash with

Combines with Yang Earth — The Great Mountain. Yin Water combines with Yang Earth (戊癸合). The mountain holding the rain — the partner who receives your feeling and gives it somewhere to rest.

Clashes with Yin Fire — The Candle Flame. Yin Water clashes with Yin Fire (丁癸沖). Rain and candle under one roof: an awkward pairing that works only with distance and respect for each other's sensitivity.

Two Day Masters are only the first line of a compatibility reading. Put in both birth dates and the match page adds what you are to each other in the Ten Gods and whether your elements fill each other's gaps.

Strong, balanced or weak?

The same Day Master reads differently depending on how well it is supported by the other seven characters.

  • Strong — A monsoon. Everything grows around you — and floods. Ration the giving.
  • Balanced — Gentle, clear, persistent — the Dew that becomes a river without anyone noticing.
  • Weak — A single drop. Stop evaporating in other people's weather; collect yourself somewhere cool.
