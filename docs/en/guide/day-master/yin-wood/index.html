Sajucheop library · Grass that rides the wind

The one who does not break — it goes around.

Who you are

You seep in rather than charge. You read rooms and people quickly, bend instead of snapping, and end up exactly where you wanted to be. Anyone who calls the Vine weak has never watched it climb a cliff. Your softness is a strategy, and it works.

Strengths

  • Adaptability that survives anywhere — new job, new city, new crowd, half the settling-in time.
  • A fine antenna for what people feel before they say it.

Watch out for

  • Saying no is hard, so you carry losses you never agreed to.
  • If you only look for something to lean on, your own strength grows late.

In love

You wrap around a partner slowly and completely. Once tangled, letting go means pulling out roots — which is why a Vine's breakups ache so long. You flourish with someone steady enough to climb and kind enough not to notice you are doing it.

At work

Roles built on relationships and timing: sales, diplomacy, care, design, anything where reading people is the skill. You rise highest inside a good team or beside a strong partner.

Chemistry — who you combine and clash with

Combines with Yang Metal — The Raw Blade. Yin Wood combines with Yang Metal (乙庚合). The vine has found a pillar — a sturdy partner to climb, and together you go higher than either alone.

Clashes with Yin Metal — The Polished Gem. Yin Wood clashes with Yin Metal (乙辛沖). The Gem's sharp edge cuts easily; a little distance keeps both of you elegant.

Two Day Masters are only the first line of a compatibility reading. Put in both birth dates and the match page adds what you are to each other in the Ten Gods and whether your elements fill each other's gaps.

Strong, balanced or weak?

The same Day Master reads differently depending on how well it is supported by the other seven characters.

  • Strong — A vine in full summer — spreading everywhere at once. Pick two walls, not ten.
  • Balanced — Soft where it needs to be, rooted where it counts — the most livable version of the Vine.
  • Weak — A seedling in the shade. Find the wall first; the climbing takes care of itself.
