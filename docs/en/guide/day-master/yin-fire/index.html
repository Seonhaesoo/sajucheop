The Candle Flame

Sajucheop library · A single point of light in the dark

Quiet — and burning to the very end.

Who you are

You shine in the deep night, not the bright afternoon. When something catches you, you go long and deep, and you keep the people beside you warm with real attention. The surface is gentle; the wick underneath is hotter than anyone guesses.

Strengths

  • A craftsman's temperament — one field, dug to the bottom.
  • A kind, precise warmth that genuinely revives people.

Watch out for

  • You are burning inside while smiling outside.
  • Burnout gives no warning with you — it simply arrives.

In love

You light one person for a long time. Deep beats wide; you notice the haircut, the tired voice, the thing left unsaid. Your one flaw is forgetting your own wick is shortening — choose someone who asks "and how are you?" without being prompted.

At work

Any craft where care is the product: healing, counseling, cooking, fine work with the hands, research that takes years. Small, dense teams suit you better than big open floors.

Chemistry — who you combine and clash with

Combines with Yang Water — The Open Sea. Yin Fire combines with Yang Water (丁壬合). A lantern floating on the river — romance and depth meeting, the kind of bond that lasts.

Clashes with Yin Water — The Morning Dew. Yin Fire clashes with Yin Water (丁癸沖). A candle in the rain: your flame is easily dampened, so both of you need the courtesy of guarding each other's temperature.

Two Day Masters are only the first line of a compatibility reading. Put in both birth dates and the match page adds what you are to each other in the Ten Gods and whether your elements fill each other's gaps.

Strong, balanced or weak?

The same Day Master reads differently depending on how well it is supported by the other seven characters.

  • Strong — A bonfire in a candle's body. Intense, magnetic — ration the intensity or it eats you.
  • Balanced — Steady, warm, unhurried — the Flame that lights a whole house one room at a time.
  • Weak — A match in the wind. Protect the flame first: sleep, boundaries, one thing at a time.
