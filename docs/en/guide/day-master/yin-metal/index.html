The one who wins on detail.

Who you are

You cannot stand "good enough". Your eye is sharp and your bar is high, and anything that passed through your hands shows it. You keep scratches in memory for years — and that same pride is what makes you a masterpiece rather than a stone.

Strengths

  • An eye nobody can fool.
  • The finish — you make the last two percent shine.

Watch out for

  • Your standards exhaust you more than anyone else.
  • You store resentment in a very well-organized drawer.

In love

You love selectively and completely. "Anything is fine" is never fine; the restaurant, the playlist, the way they text all matter. Forgiven is not forgotten with you — choose someone careful, and be a little less careful yourself.

At work

Precision and taste: finance, jewelry, aesthetics, editing, quality control, medicine, anything where being exactly right is the whole job. A personal brand suits you.

Chemistry — who you combine and clash with

Combines with Yang Fire — The Midday Sun. Yin Metal combines with Yang Fire (丙辛合). A jewel catching the light — the partner who shows the world what you are worth.

Clashes with Yin Wood — The Winding Vine. Yin Metal clashes with Yin Wood (乙辛沖). Different grains; you can frustrate each other. Accept the difference and you fill each other's gaps instead.

Two Day Masters are only the first line of a compatibility reading. Put in both birth dates and the match page adds what you are to each other in the Ten Gods and whether your elements fill each other's gaps.

Strong, balanced or weak?

The same Day Master reads differently depending on how well it is supported by the other seven characters.

  • Strong — A diamond — brilliant, hard, hard to hold. Let one person see the flaw.
  • Balanced — Refined and warm — the Gem set in something human.
  • Weak — An uncut stone. Your value is real but unseen; find the light (a mentor, a stage) and the polishing follows.
