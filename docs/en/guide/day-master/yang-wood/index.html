Yang Wood Day Master —
The Tall Pine

Sajucheop library · The first tree to open the forest

If there is no road, the Pine cuts one.

Who you are

You are most yourself at the front of the group. Decisions come fast, and the moment a goal appears your body is already moving toward it. Bending is hard for you — you lose a little now and then because of it — but that straightness is exactly your brand. People trust a Pine because it does not lean.

Strengths

  • The power to turn zero into one — you are built for beginnings.
  • A word once given is a word kept. Your reliability is visible from a distance.

Watch out for

  • Changing direction is slow for you. Sometimes the detour is the faster road.
  • You cannot stand losing, so you push past the point where rest would have won.

In love

You love by leading — protecting, planning, standing in front. Partners who need a hero feel safe; partners who need a peer may feel managed. The Pine grows best beside someone who can say "sit down, I have got this" once in a while.

At work

Pioneering roles: founding, launching, opening new markets, leading a team into unknown ground. Maintenance and endless negotiation drain you; hand the second phase to someone else and go open the next door.

Chemistry — who you combine and clash with

Combines with Yin Earth — The Fertile Field. Yang Wood combines with Yin Earth (甲己合). A Pine that meets the Field has found soil to root in — each supplies precisely what the other lacks.

Clashes with Yang Metal — The Raw Blade. Yang Wood clashes with Yang Metal (甲庚沖). The axe and the tree: it hurts when they meet, yet the Blade is also what turns a Pine into timber. Handle with respect and it becomes momentum.

Two Day Masters are only the first line of a compatibility reading. Put in both birth dates and the match page adds what you are to each other in the Ten Gods and whether your elements fill each other's gaps.

Strong, balanced or weak?

The same Day Master reads differently depending on how well it is supported by the other seven characters.

  • Strong — A deep-rooted giant. Your pushing power is exceptional — keep someone close who is allowed to pull the brake.
  • Balanced — Strength and flexibility in balance — a tree that earns its place in any forest.
  • Weak — A young shoot. Choose your soil (environment) carefully and you will outgrow everyone.
