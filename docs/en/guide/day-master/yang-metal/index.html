Sajucheop library · Iron that gets stronger the more it is forged

The one who does not postpone — it cuts.

Who you are

You settle tangled problems with one stroke. Loyalty and principle are non-negotiable, and when something is wrong you say so. You do not fear collision — but the more you learn to sheathe the blade, the more of a master you become.

Strengths

  • Breakthrough power exactly when things are stuck.
  • Loyalty to the end for the people you call yours.

Watch out for

  • Your words become blades sometimes without your noticing.
  • If you treat compromise as defeat, you are the one who ends up exhausted.

In love

You are terrible at tenderness and excellent at showing up. "Did you eat?" is your love letter. Partners who read actions feel deeply protected; partners who need the words go hungry. Learn three soft sentences and use them.

At work

Fields with rules and stakes: law, military and police, surgery, engineering, competitive sport, turnarounds. Pressure sharpens you where it dulls others.

Chemistry — who you combine and clash with

Combines with Yin Wood — The Winding Vine. Yang Metal combines with Yin Wood (乙庚合). Softness wrapping around strength — the partner who gentles your edge and is grateful for your steel.

Clashes with Yang Wood — The Tall Pine. Yang Metal clashes with Yang Wood (甲庚沖). Axe versus great tree: you test each other constantly, and both come out harder for it.

Two Day Masters are only the first line of a compatibility reading. Put in both birth dates and the match page adds what you are to each other in the Ten Gods and whether your elements fill each other's gaps.

Strong, balanced or weak?

The same Day Master reads differently depending on how well it is supported by the other seven characters.

  • Strong — Tempered steel — nothing bends you. Make sure something can still touch you.
  • Balanced — Sharp and sheathed — the Blade that is trusted precisely because it does not need to be drawn.
  • Weak — Ore not yet forged. Hardship is not your enemy here; it is the forge. Stay in it a little longer.
