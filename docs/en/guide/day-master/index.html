How the ten are built

Five elements, each in a yang and a yin form, make the ten heavenly stems. The yang stems — Jia, Bing, Wu, Geng and Ren — are the outward, expansive side of their element: the tall pine, the midday sun. The yin stems — Yi, Ding, Ji, Xin and Gui — are the inward, refined side: the vine, the candle flame. Neither is better; they are two ways of being the same element.

The Day Master decides how every other character in the chart is read. The same Fire is output to a Wood person, a peer to a Fire person and pressure to a Metal person — that is the logic of the Ten Gods. Whether a Day Master is strong or weak changes the reading again, and the branch it sits on gives the 60 Day Pillars.
