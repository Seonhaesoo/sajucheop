What Bath means

Bath is the second stage, and the picture is a newborn being washed for the first time. The child is bare and everything touches it: warm water, cold air, a stranger's hands. The stem's energy on this branch is alive and growing, but it has no covering yet. Jia 甲 bathes on the Rat 子, Bing 丙 and Wu 戊 on the Rabbit 卯, Geng 庚 on the Horse 午, Ren 壬 on the Rooster 酉. The yin stems, counting backward, bathe on the season-opening branches: Yi 乙 on the Snake 巳, Ding 丁 and Ji 己 on the Monkey 申, Xin 辛 on the Pig 亥, Gui 癸 on the Tiger 寅.

The light of Bath is attractiveness. This is the stage of sensibility, taste, and the kind of charm that is not worked at. People on it are noticed and liked, and they have a feeling for beauty, mood and other people's moods that later stages lose. For the yang stems the Bath branch is the same branch as the Peach Blossom of their three-harmony frame, which is why old texts tie Bath to romance and flair. The shadow is exposure. A bare child feels every draft, and Bath energy is changeable, easily wounded, and quick to move on. Interests and affections can come and go.

Old commentaries were hard on Bath. They called it the failure of the sweet, and warned of scandal and waste. Modern readers keep the sensitivity and drop the moralizing. Bath describes a stem whose energy is real but unprotected, and the practical question is what protects it: routine, a craft, a steady partner, a chart with enough Earth or Resource to give the child a towel. Bath is also not a verdict on morals. Many artists, performers and counselors carry it, and its swings are the same swings that let them feel what others miss. The misreading is to hear the old warnings as a prophecy.

Personality when it sits under you

Four day pillars sit on Bath: Jia Zi 甲子, Yi Si 乙巳, Geng Wu 庚午 and Xin Hai 辛亥. With one of these as your day pillar, you are the person a room notices without knowing why. You have taste, you read people quickly, and you are more moved by beauty and atmosphere than you let on. You also change. Enthusiasms rise and fall, your mood answers the weather of the people around you, and you can be hurt by remarks that others would not even hear. The strength is a real gift for anything that depends on feeling: design, music, hospitality, the caring professions. The task is to build a frame that holds when the feeling drops, because it will, and to notice that being liked is not the same as being known.

With Bath on the month branch, the changeable energy shows in your working life. Careers tend to start in one place and finish in another, sometimes several others, and the early years can look scattered from outside. Inside, the thread is usually consistent: you follow what engages your senses and leave what does not. You are well liked at work and often become the person who sets the tone of an office. The challenge is the middle stretch, the years between thirty and fifty when steadiness pays more than charm. A Bath month does well to choose a field where restlessness is an asset, such as media, fashion or travel, or to pair with people who love the parts of work you find dull.

In each pillar

Year branchA Bath stage on the year branch belongs to the early years and the family background. It often describes a childhood with moves, changes or a colourful household, and an early sensitivity to how others felt. Some readers see a family line that valued appearance or the arts. It is context, not character.
Month branchOn the month branch, Bath is the energy you take to work: charm, taste and a low tolerance for boredom. Reputation comes easily and can go just as easily. Fields that reward feeling and presentation suit it. The middle years ask for a steadiness the stage does not supply on its own.
Day branchOn the day branch, Bath is your constitution and the spouse seat. You are sensitive, attractive and changeable, and you are drawn to partners who are the same. Relationships can be intense and uneven. The stage is not a sentence of infidelity, as old books suggested; it asks for honesty about what you feel.
Hour branchOn the hour branch, Bath colours the later years and the children. It points to a late chapter that stays curious and social rather than settling, and to children with charm and moods of their own. Keep something practical in hand for the years when feeling alone is not enough.

In a luck pillar or a year

A luck pillar or year in your Bath stage is a season of exposure. New people, tastes and attractions come easily; so do overspending and changes of heart. It is a good time to learn something that engages the senses and a poor time to make permanent commitments in the first flush of feeling. In 2026, the Horse year, Geng 庚 Day Masters are in Bath. For them it is a year to enjoy being noticed and sign nothing on a warm evening. Check what the Horse is for you as a Ten God before trusting the mood.

FAQ

Is Bath the same as Peach Blossom?

Not the same, but they overlap. For the yang stems the Bath branch is the Peach Blossom branch of their three-harmony frame: the Rat for Jia, the Rabbit for Bing, the Horse for Geng, the Rooster for Ren. Bath is a strength stage; Peach Blossom is a star. Where they coincide, the charm reading is stronger.

Old books say Bath means scandal. Should I worry?

No. The old warnings describe unprotected feeling in a society with little room for it. Today readers keep the sensitivity, the charm and the changeability, and leave the moralizing. A Bath day pillar is common among people who work with beauty and emotion, and it says nothing about anyone's conduct.

How do I steady a Bath day pillar?

Look for what covers the child. Earth in the chart, a Resource star, a luck pillar in Cap and Belt or Official, a job with rhythm, a partner who is calm. Structure is not the enemy of your sensitivity; it is what lets you use it without being used up by it.
