What Sickness means

Sickness is the seventh stage, and the picture is a person in later life whose body has begun to fail. The name is literal in the story and figurative in the reading. Nothing about this stage describes anyone's health; it describes a stem whose energy on this branch is low, inward and tender. Jia 甲 meets Sickness on the Snake 巳, Bing 丙 and Wu 戊 on the Monkey 申, Geng 庚 on the Pig 亥, Ren 壬 on the Tiger 寅. Counting backward, Yi 乙 sickens on the Rat 子, Ding 丁 and Ji 己 on the Rabbit 卯, Xin 辛 on the Horse 午, Gui 癸 on the Rooster 酉.

The light of Sickness is empathy. A person who has been weak knows what weakness feels like, and the energy on this stage is unusually sensitive to pain, mood and need in others. Readers associate it with care, counsel, nursing, teaching the struggling, and with a fine, anxious intelligence that notices what the strong walk past. The shadow is the cost of that noticing. Sickness energy worries, tires, takes things to heart and needs more rest than it is given. It can also hesitate, turning the fear of being hurt into a habit of not trying, and it can mistake sympathy for a plan.

Sickness sits directly across the circle from Birth, and the pairing is the key to reading it. Where Birth is fresh and outward, Sickness is worn and inward; both are gentle, and neither is strong. The misreading is the obvious one, and it is worth saying plainly: a Sickness day pillar is not a forecast of illness, a Sickness year is not a warning about your body, and the stage says nothing a doctor would recognise. What it says is that the stem is tired on that branch and works best when it is allowed to feel rather than fight. Many gifted people carry it, and carry it well.

Personality when it sits under you

Six day pillars sit on Sickness: Bing Shen 丙申, Wu Shen 戊申, Ren Yin 壬寅, Ding Mao 丁卯, Ji Mao 己卯 and Gui You 癸酉. If yours is one of these, you are finely tuned. You feel a room before you enter it, you know when a friend is not all right before they say so, and you carry other people's troubles home with you. That makes you good at the work that depends on understanding: care, counselling, teaching, writing, any craft that needs a delicate hand. It also makes you tired. Your nerves work harder than most, and the practical task is to build rest into your life as a fixed cost rather than a reward. Learn also to let a worry pass through you instead of settling in.

With Sickness on the month branch, the sensitivity goes into your working life. You are drawn to work that helps, heals or explains, and you are often the person a team turns to when something has gone wrong with a person rather than a plan. Public life can be tiring for you, and the middle years, when ambition is loudest around you, can feel like a competition you did not enter. Readers advise a Sickness month to choose depth over breadth: one field known thoroughly, a workload that leaves margin, and colleagues who do not mistake gentleness for absence. A chart with strong Resource, or a Birth or Official branch elsewhere, gives this placement the support it needs, and it then does very well.

In each pillar

Year branchSickness on the year branch belongs to the early years and the family background. It may describe a childhood with illness in the house, a parent or grandparent who needed care, or simply an early sensitivity to the moods around you. It is context, and it explains tenderness rather than predicting anything.
Month branchOn the month branch, Sickness is the energy you bring to society: perception, care and a low tolerance for combat. Helping professions and careful crafts suit it. Guard your reserves in the middle years. With supporting stars elsewhere the placement becomes a real gift for understanding people.
Day branchOn the day branch, Sickness is your constitution and the spouse seat. You are sensitive, thoughtful and easily drained, and you often choose partners who need looking after, or who look after you. The marriage is close and attentive. It is not a health forecast, for you or for them.
Hour branchOn the hour branch, Sickness suggests later years that turn inward: quieter, more reflective, more attentive to family than to the world. Children may be delicate or need extra care. The last chapter asks for good habits started early and for company that restores you.

In a luck pillar or a year

A luck pillar or year in your Sickness stage is a season of low reserves. Energy runs shorter than usual, worries loom larger, and the wise use of the period is to care rather than to campaign: for yourself, for people who need it, for work that wants a delicate touch. It is a poor time to force outcomes and a good time to study or to mend a relationship. In 2026, the Horse year, Xin 辛 Day Masters are in Sickness; for them the year rewards margin and a lighter load. None of this describes anyone's body.

FAQ

Does a Sickness day pillar mean I will be sickly?

No. The name is the seventh scene in a story about a life; it is not a diagnosis and not a forecast. Sickness describes a stem whose energy is low and inward on that branch, which reads as sensitivity and a need for rest. For questions about your health, ask a doctor, not a chart.

What is Sickness good for?

Understanding. People with Sickness prominent tend to notice what the robust miss, and they are drawn to care, counsel, teaching and detailed crafts. The stage also brings a fine sense of when to stop, which stronger stages lack. It is a quiet strength, and a real one.

Is Sickness the opposite of Birth?

On the table, yes: they sit six branches apart, so the Sickness branch always clashes with the Birth branch of the same stem. Jia is born on the Pig and sickens on the Snake. Readers use the pairing to remember that both are gentle stages, one facing outward and one facing in.
