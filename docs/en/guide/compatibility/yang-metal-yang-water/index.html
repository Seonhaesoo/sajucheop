The Raw Blade (Geng) meets The Open Sea (Ren)

You nourish them (상생) — as a spring rises from rock. Your Metal feeds their Water. One side pushes steadily from behind; the giving and receiving are clear.

First impression

Your energy feeds theirs. You give, they receive: they brighten when you meet, and you find meaning in watching it. Keep the giver from tiring and the receiver saying thank you — that cycle is the relationship's fuel.

Same polarity: familiar and comfortable, but the push-and-pull has to be created on purpose.

They are Eating God to you Shi Shen · Output

They are Eating God to you — the one who draws your talent out. Words come easily and ideas flow in front of them. Eating, making and enjoying multiply together; life gets richer.

You are Indirect Resource to them Pian Yin · Resource

You are Indirect Resource to them — the one who inspires. They leave a conversation with you holding a new thought.

In love

You love by tending. They draw stability and strength from you; you draw joy from growing them. Give only, though, and one day you ask "who fills me?" Practice receiving too.

At work and in partnership

You are the one pushing from behind — mentor and junior, backer and builder. When they succeed, your contribution can vanish from view; keep a record of what you added.

To make it last

The joy of giving is real, but once it becomes duty the relationship gets heavy. Sit in the receiving seat sometimes.
