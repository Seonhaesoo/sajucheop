Who you are

Fire forging metal. Direct Officer below values rules and honor and breeds loyalty, while Bath adds charm and moodiness. Trials sharpen this pillar — early hardship becomes later skill. Hot and decisive, with a temperature that rises and falls quickly.

What the Horse branch says

The Day Branch is your inner room and your spouse seat. The Horse (午, Wu) carries 3 hidden stems:

Yang Fire (丙, Bing) — Seven Killings

Yin Fire (丁, Ding) — Direct Officer main qi

With Direct Officer in the Day Branch, the spouse seat holds duty and structure. Upright partners appear; traditionally read as good marriage luck for women.

Twelve-stage: Bath — the washing place. Charm and flair, wide emotional swings; loved easily, shaken easily.
