Who you are

A candle illuminating a jewel. Indirect Wealth below gives money sense; Birth makes beginnings good and help easy to find. Delicate, artistic, with a deep eye for beautiful things. Like a small flame that makes a jewel shine, it has the gift of making others look better — so people gather.

The Day Master is the "you" of the chart — here, The Candle Flame: Quiet — and burning to the very end. What gives Ding You its own color is the room the Rooster beneath it offers.

What the Rooster branch says

The Day Branch is your inner room and your spouse seat. The Rooster (酉, You) carries 2 hidden stems:

Yang Metal (庚, Geng) — Direct Wealth
Yin Metal (辛, Xin) — Indirect Wealth main qi

With Indirect Wealth in the Day Branch, partner and money move together. Sociable, generous partners appear; for men, romantic connections tend to be wide.

Twelve-stage: Birth — newborn energy. Strong at beginnings, well supported by people, and youthful long after youth.

In love

Romantic and refined. Drawn to shared taste and mood, and loves by making a partner shine.

At work

Design, finance, beauty, food — taste and delicacy as skill. The eye for good things is the asset.
