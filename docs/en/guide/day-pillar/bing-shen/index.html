The sun over metal.

Who you are

The sun over metal. Indirect Wealth below makes it sociable, active, and placed where money moves. Water hidden in the Monkey sometimes dims the sun, and Sickness adds sensitivity. Brilliant and wide-reaching, but hard to keep in one place — relationships and work can go wide and shallow.

The Day Master is the "you" of the chart — here, The Midday Sun: The one who cannot hide, and lights everyone up. What gives Bing Shen its own color is the room the Monkey beneath it offers.

What the Monkey branch says

The Day Branch is your inner room and your spouse seat. The Monkey (申, Shen) carries 3 hidden stems:

Yang Earth (戊, Wu) — Eating God
Yang Water (壬, Ren) — Seven Killings
Yang Metal (庚, Geng) — Indirect Wealth main qi

With Indirect Wealth in the Day Branch, partner and money move together. Sociable, generous partners appear; for men, romantic connections tend to be wide.

Twelve-stage: Sickness — the sensitive place. Deep feeling and empathy for others' pain; needs habits that protect energy and nerves.

In love

Dazzling and charming. The heart moves fast and so does its whims; slowing down deliberately is what lasts.

At work

Business, sales, broadcasting, marketing — stages where people and money circulate widely. Strong at starting and scaling.
