Who you are

Grass growing over a field. Seven Killings below keeps a constant low tension, and with it strong diligence and duty. Sickness makes it sensitive to others' feelings; unable to refuse, the field fills with other people's weeds. Soft, and quietly enduring for a long time.

The Day Master is the "you" of the chart — here, The Fertile Field: The one beside whom things grow. What gives Ji Mao its own color is the room the Rabbit beneath it offers.

What the Rabbit branch says

The Day Branch is your inner room and your spouse seat. The Rabbit (卯, Mao) carries 2 hidden stems:

Yang Wood (甲, Jia) — Direct Officer
Yin Wood (乙, Yi) — Seven Killings main qi

With Seven Killings in the Day Branch, a partner keeps you on your toes. You grow beside strong people but can feel pressed; for women, charismatic partners appear.

Twelve-stage: Sickness — the sensitive place. Deep feeling and empathy for others' pain; needs habits that protect energy and nerves.

In love

Devoted. Easily loses itself matching a partner — check that the giving flows back.

At work

Service, nursing, education, welfare — caring for people shows its worth. Works long in organizations that recognize it.
