Who you are

Great water blocked by autumn earth — a Kui Gang pillar. Seven Killings below means living with duty and pressure, and Cap and Belt turns it into achievement. Metal and fire inside the Dog bring wisdom and wealth together; brilliant and charismatic. Like dammed water gathering force, it becomes big when it waits for its moment.

The Day Master is the "you" of the chart — here, The Open Sea: The one who flows toward the wider place. What gives Ren Xu its own color is the room the Dog beneath it offers.

What the Dog branch says

The Day Branch is your inner room and your spouse seat. The Dog (戌, Xu) carries 3 hidden stems:

Yin Metal (辛, Xin) — Direct Resource
Yin Fire (丁, Ding) — Direct Wealth
Yang Earth (戊, Wu) — Seven Killings main qi

With Seven Killings in the Day Branch, a partner keeps you on your toes. You grow beside strong people but can feel pressed; for women, charismatic partners appear.

Twelve-stage: Cap and Belt — dressing for society. Strong ambition and face; invests real effort in becoming presentable and capable.

In love

A leader. Takes the lead in relationships too; balance comes with a partner as solid as itself.

At work

Law, management, military and police, medicine — authority and judgment. The bigger the responsibility, the bigger the skill.
