Who you are

Soft wood keeping a small flame alive. Delicate, artistic, the first to notice a subtle change. Indirect Resource brings rich imagination and a deep inner world; affection makes this pillar care for people for years. That sensitivity cuts both ways — a small word lingers for a long time.

The Day Master is the "you" of the chart — here, The Candle Flame: Quiet — and burning to the very end. What gives Ding Mao its own color is the room the Rabbit beneath it offers.

What the Rabbit branch says

The Day Branch is your inner room and your spouse seat. The Rabbit (卯, Mao) carries 2 hidden stems:

Yang Wood (甲, Jia) — Direct Resource
Yin Wood (乙, Yi) — Indirect Resource main qi

With Indirect Resource in the Day Branch, the spouse seat is unusual. Deep mental connection with an original thinker; practical life needs mutual care.

Twelve-stage: Sickness — the sensitive place. Deep feeling and empathy for others' pain; needs habits that protect energy and nerves.

In love

Romantic and devoted. Falls deeply for one person and is wounded deeply too, so opening up takes time.

At work

Design, writing, counseling, crafts — where delicacy is the skill. Small, dense teams over big floors.
