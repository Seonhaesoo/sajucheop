Who you are

A spring flowing inside a mountain. Direct Wealth below gives practical sense; the water inside the Rat becomes the mountain's wealth, so property and saving come naturally. Conception adds potential; the outside is easygoing, the arithmetic exact. A realist who puts stability first.

The Day Master is the "you" of the chart — here, The Great Mountain: The one everybody leans on to rest. What gives Wu Zi its own color is the room the Rat beneath it offers.

What the Rat branch says

The Day Branch is your inner room and your spouse seat. The Rat (子, Zi) carries 2 hidden stems:

Yang Water (壬, Ren) — Indirect Wealth
Yin Water (癸, Gui) — Direct Wealth main qi

With Direct Wealth in the Day Branch, the spouse seat is stable. Thrifty, realistic partners arrive; traditionally read as good marriage luck for men.

Twelve-stage: Conception — the seed is set. Huge potential, no fixed shape yet; can become anything, but needs a protective environment.

In love

At work

Finance, property, distribution, management — handling assets. Steady accumulation over big gambles.
