Who you are

Sunset over the sea — water and fire meeting in the "completed" pattern. Direct Wealth below brings practical sense and money feel, and plenty of affection for people. Earth inside the Horse adds balance; Conception adds possibility. Large emotional waves, and just as much warmth.

The Day Master is the "you" of the chart — here, The Open Sea: The one who flows toward the wider place. What gives Ren Wu its own color is the room the Horse beneath it offers.

What the Horse branch says

The Day Branch is your inner room and your spouse seat. The Horse (午, Wu) carries 3 hidden stems:

Yang Fire (丙, Bing) — Indirect Wealth
Yin Earth (己, Ji) — Direct Officer
Yin Fire (丁, Ding) — Direct Wealth main qi

With Direct Wealth in the Day Branch, the spouse seat is stable. Thrifty, realistic partners arrive; traditionally read as good marriage luck for men.

Twelve-stage: Conception — the seed is set. Huge potential, no fixed shape yet; can become anything, but needs a protective environment.

In love

Traditionally read as good marriage luck. Affectionate and attentive; once given, the heart stays.

At work

Finance, trade, service, distribution — where people and money flow together. Keeps clients through warmth.
