Water pressing on fire.

Who you are

Water pressing on fire. Direct Officer below values duty and face — bright outside, restrained inside. Conception gives large potential, and having guarded its flame through winter water, this pillar carries a grown-up quality. Strong control of feeling; the inside rarely shows.

The Day Master is the "you" of the chart — here, The Midday Sun: The one who cannot hide, and lights everyone up. What gives Bing Zi its own color is the room the Rat beneath it offers.

What the Rat branch says

The Day Branch is your inner room and your spouse seat. The Rat (子, Zi) carries 2 hidden stems:

Yang Water (壬, Ren) — Seven Killings
Yin Water (癸, Gui) — Direct Officer main qi

With Direct Officer in the Day Branch, the spouse seat holds duty and structure. Upright partners appear; traditionally read as good marriage luck for women.

Twelve-stage: Conception — the seed is set. Huge potential, no fixed shape yet; can become anything, but needs a protective environment.

In love

Well-mannered and self-governed. Builds trust rather than passion, and expects manners in return.

At work

Public service, organizations, broadcasting, education — shines within a framework. The more structure, the steadier the performance.
