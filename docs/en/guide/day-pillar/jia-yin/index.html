Who you are

Tree on tree — the pillar of self-reliance. Friend below gives independence and drive; Prosperity means standing without leaning on anyone. Fire and earth inside the Tiger add wealth and expression, so honesty and leadership come together. Being in front is natural.

The Day Master is the "you" of the chart — here, The Tall Pine: If there is no road, the Pine cuts one. What gives Jia Yin its own color is the room the Tiger beneath it offers.

What the Tiger branch says

The Day Branch is your inner room and your spouse seat. The Tiger (寅, Yin) carries 3 hidden stems:

Yang Earth (戊, Wu) — Indirect Wealth
Yang Fire (丙, Bing) — Eating God
Yang Wood (甲, Jia) — Friend main qi

With Friend in the Day Branch, the spouse seat holds "someone like me". Marriage feels like partnership between equals; the practice is taking turns with the wheel.

Twelve-stage: Prosperity — standing on one's own feet. Independent, self-earning, self-spending, leaning on no one.

In love

Independent. Lasts in relationships that respect each other's territory, and grows sturdier by learning consideration.

At work

Founding, leadership, education, pioneering — opening boards by itself. Shines under its own name more than under someone else's.
