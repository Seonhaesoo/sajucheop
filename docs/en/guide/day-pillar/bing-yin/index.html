Who you are

Wood feeding fire — a sun just risen. Optimistic and curious, quick to start anywhere, quickly the center of any room. The Indirect Resource angle gives an original way of thinking and treats learning as play. Endings, though, get as little attention as sunsets: many things begun, fewer finished.

The Day Master is the "you" of the chart — here, The Midday Sun: The one who cannot hide, and lights everyone up. What gives Bing Yin its own color is the room the Tiger beneath it offers.

What the Tiger branch says

The Day Branch is your inner room and your spouse seat. The Tiger (寅, Yin) carries 3 hidden stems:

Yang Earth (戊, Wu) — Eating God
Yang Fire (丙, Bing) — Friend
Yang Wood (甲, Jia) — Indirect Resource main qi

With Indirect Resource in the Day Branch, the spouse seat is unusual. Deep mental connection with an original thinker; practical life needs mutual care.

Twelve-stage: Birth — newborn energy. Strong at beginnings, well supported by people, and youthful long after youth.

In love

At work

Teaching, media, the early phase of a startup — anything that needs a spark. Hand the maintenance phase to someone else.
