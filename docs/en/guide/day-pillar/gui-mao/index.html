Dew raising a sprout.

Who you are

Dew raising a sprout. Eating God below gives rich talent and expression; Birth makes beginnings good and help easy. Gentle, artistic, with a power to put people at ease. Like spring rain, it seeps in quietly and makes its surroundings grow.

The Day Master is the "you" of the chart — here, The Morning Dew: The one who sees everything and says nothing. What gives Gui Mao its own color is the room the Rabbit beneath it offers.

What the Rabbit branch says

The Day Branch is your inner room and your spouse seat. The Rabbit (卯, Mao) carries 2 hidden stems:

Yang Wood (甲, Jia) — Hurting Officer
Yin Wood (乙, Yi) — Eating God main qi

With Eating God in the Day Branch, the spouse seat is abundant. A couple that cooks, makes and enjoys things together; laughter is frequent at home.

Twelve-stage: Birth — newborn energy. Strong at beginnings, well supported by people, and youthful long after youth.

In love

Affectionate and popular. Loves by giving comfort, and is genuinely glad at a partner's growth.

At work

Art, education, food, service — raising people and delighting them. Warmth is the skill.
