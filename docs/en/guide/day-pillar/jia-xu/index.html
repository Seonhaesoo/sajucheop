Who you are

A tree on autumn soil. Indirect Wealth below gives realism and a feel for money, with a wide range of action. Fire and metal inside the Dog make loyalty and stubbornness strong — a job accepted is a job finished. A Nurture pillar: a late bloomer who grows by raising others.

The Day Master is the "you" of the chart — here, The Tall Pine: If there is no road, the Pine cuts one. What gives Jia Xu its own color is the room the Dog beneath it offers.

What the Dog branch says

The Day Branch is your inner room and your spouse seat. The Dog (戌, Xu) carries 3 hidden stems:

Yin Metal (辛, Xin) — Direct Officer
Yin Fire (丁, Ding) — Hurting Officer
Yang Earth (戊, Wu) — Indirect Wealth main qi

With Indirect Wealth in the Day Branch, partner and money move together. Sociable, generous partners appear; for men, romantic connections tend to be wide.

Twelve-stage: Nurture — being raised. A late bloomer with a gift for raising others.

In love

Loves through responsibility. Feels affection in looking after a partner, and proves it with action rather than words.

At work

Business, sales, property, distribution — the ground where money moves. Gathers people and enlarges the board.
