Metal cutting wood.

Who you are

Metal cutting wood. Indirect Wealth below makes it active and large-scale, living for the pleasure of pioneering. Extinction brings swings in fortune, but recovery is just as fast. Fire and earth inside the Tiger give drive — earns boldly, spends boldly.

The Day Master is the "you" of the chart — here, The Raw Blade: The one who does not postpone — it cuts. What gives Geng Yin its own color is the room the Tiger beneath it offers.

What the Tiger branch says

The Day Branch is your inner room and your spouse seat. The Tiger (寅, Yin) carries 3 hidden stems:

Yang Earth (戊, Wu) — Indirect Resource
Yang Fire (丙, Bing) — Seven Killings
Yang Wood (甲, Jia) — Indirect Wealth main qi

With Indirect Wealth in the Day Branch, partner and money move together. Sociable, generous partners appear; for men, romantic connections tend to be wide.

Twelve-stage: Extinction — cut off, then reconnected. Swings between extremes, and recovers with matching force. Do not fear change.

In love

Bold and possessive. Wants to lead and take responsibility; fits someone who runs alongside rather than someone who needs space.

At work

Business, distribution, military and police, construction — moving in the field. Better opening boards than sitting at desks.
