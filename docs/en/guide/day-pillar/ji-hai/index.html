A field at the water's edge. Direct Wealth below gives practicality and realism; the wood inside the Pig governs the field, so both Officer and Resource sit in the spouse seat. Conception adds potential and gentleness. Receives softly, harvests quietly.

What the Pig branch says

The Day Branch is your inner room and your spouse seat. The Pig (亥, Hai) carries 3 hidden stems:

With Direct Wealth in the Day Branch, the spouse seat is stable. Thrifty, realistic partners arrive; traditionally read as good marriage luck for men.

Twelve-stage: Conception — the seed is set. Huge potential, no fixed shape yet; can become anything, but needs a protective environment.
