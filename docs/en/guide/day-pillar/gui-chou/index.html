Who you are

Dew on winter soil. Seven Killings below gives duty and endurance; kindred water and metal inside the Ox give roots and help together. Cap and Belt adds social ambition; diligent and weighty. Quietly endures and builds its own place.

The Day Master is the "you" of the chart — here, The Morning Dew: The one who sees everything and says nothing. What gives Gui Chou its own color is the room the Ox beneath it offers.

What the Ox branch says

The Day Branch is your inner room and your spouse seat. The Ox (丑, Chou) carries 3 hidden stems:

Yin Water (癸, Gui) — Friend
Yin Metal (辛, Xin) — Indirect Resource
Yin Earth (己, Ji) — Seven Killings main qi

With Seven Killings in the Day Branch, a partner keeps you on your toes. You grow beside strong people but can feel pressed; for women, charismatic partners appear.

Twelve-stage: Cap and Belt — dressing for society. Strong ambition and face; invests real effort in becoming presentable and capable.

In love

Devoted. Loves through responsibility; learning to lean on a partner instead of shouldering hard things alone helps.

At work

Administration, research, medicine, religion — patience and conviction. The type that stays and earns trust.
