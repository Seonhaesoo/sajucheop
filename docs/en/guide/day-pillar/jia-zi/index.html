Who you are

A tree with abundant water under its roots. Quick to learn and deep in thought — this pillar understands first and moves second. The straight Yang Wood nature is softened by Direct Resource wisdom, and many grow up loved by elders. The water makes waves, though: feelings run high, and the mind can outrun the body.

The Day Master is the "you" of the chart — here, The Tall Pine: If there is no road, the Pine cuts one. What gives Jia Zi its own color is the room the Rat beneath it offers.

What the Rat branch says

The Day Branch is your inner room and your spouse seat. The Rat (子, Zi) carries 2 hidden stems:

Yang Water (壬, Ren) — Indirect Resource
Yin Water (癸, Gui) — Direct Resource main qi

With Direct Resource in the Day Branch, a partner holds you. Someone to lean on without explaining, like a mother's care — just keep dependence from growing too long.

Twelve-stage: Bath — the washing place. Charm and flair, wide emotional swings; loved easily, shaken easily.

In love

Weak for intellectual attraction. Conversation opens the heart, and the wish to be looked after lasts a long time.

At work

Planning, teaching, research, writing — anything where understanding is the product. Starts late, goes far.
