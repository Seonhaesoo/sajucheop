Who you are

Dew on summer soil. Seven Killings below keeps pressure constant, and Tomb gives the patience to endure and store it. Fire and wood inside the Goat make the inside hot and sharp; strong duty means hard jobs get taken on personally. Introverted, careful — the effort rarely shows.

The Day Master is the "you" of the chart — here, The Morning Dew: The one who sees everything and says nothing. What gives Gui Wei its own color is the room the Goat beneath it offers.

What the Goat branch says

The Day Branch is your inner room and your spouse seat. The Goat (未, Wei) carries 3 hidden stems:

Yin Fire (丁, Ding) — Indirect Wealth
Yin Wood (乙, Yi) — Eating God
Yin Earth (己, Ji) — Seven Killings main qi

With Seven Killings in the Day Branch, a partner keeps you on your toes. You grow beside strong people but can feel pressed; for women, charismatic partners appear.

Twelve-stage: Tomb — entering the storehouse. Collects, stores, researches; rarely shows what is inside.

In love

Devoted. Easily mistakes endurance for love; if sacrifice runs too long the relationship dries, so talk it out.

At work

Research, administration, medicine, accounting — thoroughness and patience. The slow road to expertise.
