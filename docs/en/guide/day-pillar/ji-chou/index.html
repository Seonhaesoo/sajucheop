Who you are

A field in winter. Friend below gives stubbornness and independence; Tomb gives an unusual power to endure and store. Water and metal inside the Ox make it cool and careful — steady and silent. Few words, but every accepted task gets finished, so trust piles up over time.

The Day Master is the "you" of the chart — here, The Fertile Field: The one beside whom things grow. What gives Ji Chou its own color is the room the Ox beneath it offers.

What the Ox branch says

The Day Branch is your inner room and your spouse seat. The Ox (丑, Chou) carries 3 hidden stems:

Yin Water (癸, Gui) — Indirect Wealth
Yin Metal (辛, Xin) — Eating God
Yin Earth (己, Ji) — Friend main qi

With Friend in the Day Branch, the spouse seat holds "someone like me". Marriage feels like partnership between equals; the practice is taking turns with the wheel.

Twelve-stage: Tomb — entering the storehouse. Collects, stores, researches; rarely shows what is inside.

In love

Short on words, long on trust. Shows the heart by staying rather than by speaking.

At work

Agriculture, construction, accounting, research — consistency as skill. The type that stays and becomes the root of an organization.
