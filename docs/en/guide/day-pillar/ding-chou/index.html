Who you are

A small flame warming winter earth. Eating God gives handcraft and expression; Tomb gives the power to research and store. Water and metal inside the Ox make it cool and careful — an artisan type who digs one field for years. Introverted on the surface, speaking through what it makes.

The Day Master is the "you" of the chart — here, The Candle Flame: Quiet — and burning to the very end. What gives Ding Chou its own color is the room the Ox beneath it offers.

What the Ox branch says

The Day Branch is your inner room and your spouse seat. The Ox (丑, Chou) carries 3 hidden stems:

Yin Water (癸, Gui) — Seven Killings
Yin Metal (辛, Xin) — Indirect Wealth
Yin Earth (己, Ji) — Eating God main qi

With Eating God in the Day Branch, the spouse seat is abundant. A couple that cooks, makes and enjoys things together; laughter is frequent at home.

Twelve-stage: Tomb — entering the storehouse. Collects, stores, researches; rarely shows what is inside.

In love

Subtle and long-lasting. Loves through daily care rather than grand confessions, and notices small changes in a partner.

At work

Cooking, crafts, research, accounting — hands and concentration. The quiet workshop produces the biggest results.
