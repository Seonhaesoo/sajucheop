Who you are

Sunlight striking dew. Direct Wealth below gives practicality and brightness; earth, metal and fire inside the Snake put wealth, honor and learning in one seat. Conception adds potential — small but sparkling. Precise counting behind a soft face.

The Day Master is the "you" of the chart — here, The Morning Dew: The one who sees everything and says nothing. What gives Gui Si its own color is the room the Snake beneath it offers.

What the Snake branch says

The Day Branch is your inner room and your spouse seat. The Snake (巳, Si) carries 3 hidden stems:

Yang Earth (戊, Wu) — Direct Officer
Yang Metal (庚, Geng) — Direct Resource
Yang Fire (丙, Bing) — Direct Wealth main qi

With Direct Wealth in the Day Branch, the spouse seat is stable. Thrifty, realistic partners arrive; traditionally read as good marriage luck for men.

Twelve-stage: Conception — the seed is set. Huge potential, no fixed shape yet; can become anything, but needs a protective environment.

In love

At work

Finance, service, education, accounting — thoroughness and kindness together. Builds trust as an asset.
