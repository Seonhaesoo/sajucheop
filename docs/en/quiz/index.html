The ten natures

Five elements, each in a yang (outward) and a yin (inward) form, make the ten Day Masters of a saju chart. The quiz scores your answers by element and by polarity; the real thing is read from the heavenly stem of your birth day.

FAQ

How accurate is the quiz?

It is a temperament guess, for fun. Each answer adds points to one of the five elements and to yang or yin, and the highest scores pick a Day Master. Your real one comes from your birth day and never changes — the interesting part is comparing the two.

What is a Day Master?

The heavenly stem of your birth day — the one character of eight in a saju chart that stands for you. There are ten, from Yang Wood to Yin Water, and everything else in a reading is interpreted in relation to it. The ten Day Masters →

Is this like MBTI?

Both sort people into types, but MBTI comes from a questionnaire and saju from a birth moment. This quiz borrows MBTI’s method to guess a saju type; the calculator gives you the real one without a single question.
