For the Tiger, 2027 is a year of output. Your Wood feeds the year’s Fire, so what you create, say and show gets noticed. Nothing in the branches blocks you; the only question is whether you spend energy — and money — as fast as you make it.

Overall

Your sign has neither a harmony nor a clash with the Goat, so 2027 is calm on the surface. The character of your year comes from how your element meets the year’s Fire and Earth — a quieter influence, but a real one.

For a Wood sign, the year’s Fire is output and its Earth is wealth: you express more, produce more and have more chances to earn — and to spend.

Love

A calm year for relationships. Small, consistent gestures count for more than grand ones. Tigers court openly and like to lead. They are loyal and passionate, but feeling controlled will make them bolt.

Money

Steady. A good year to build savings and pay down debt.

Work

Keep building what you started. Progress is gradual but real, and it compounds. Tigers thrive when they can pioneer — founding, leading, selling a vision. Repetitive work drains them fast.

Health

No particular weak point this year — keep your routine and it stays that way.
