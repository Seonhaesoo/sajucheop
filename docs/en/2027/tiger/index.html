Overall

Nothing on the wheel pushes the Tiger around in 2027, which means the year takes whatever shape you give it. Your Wood feeds the year’s Fire, and that is the whole story: what you make, say, teach or show travels further than it did last year, and people who had not noticed you start to. The limiting factor is not permission or opportunity — it is supply. Wood burns bright when it feeds a fire, and a Tiger who says yes to everything in spring has nothing left to give by autumn.

Use the shape of the year. Your own month (Feb 4 – Mar 5) and the spring run through the Rabbit month (Mar 6 – Apr 4) and the Dragon month (Apr 5 – May 5) are the easiest stretch for getting something off the ground; the Horse month (Jun 6 – Jul 6) is the loudest and best for anything public. The Snake month (May 6 – Jun 5), the Monkey month (Aug 8 – Sep 7) and the Rooster month (Sep 8 – Oct 7) reward tightening rather than launching, and the Dog month (Oct 8 – Nov 7) and the Pig month (Nov 8 – Dec 6) are made for closing deals and finishing well.

Your sign has neither a harmony nor a clash with the Goat, so 2027 is calm on the surface. The character of your year comes from how your element meets the year’s Fire and Earth — a quieter influence, but a real one.

For a Wood sign, the year’s Fire is output and its Earth is wealth: you express more, produce more and have more chances to earn — and to spend.

Love

A Tiger courts in the open and likes to lead, and in a year this productive the danger is obvious: the person at home ends up last on a long list. Visible people attract attention, so decide early whether you want a partner or an audience. If you are attached, protect a regular evening that belongs to the relationship and not to your projects, and let your partner see the unfinished, unimpressive parts of what you are building. If you are single, the warmest introductions come through the friends who already keep up with you — a Horse or a Pig especially.

A calm year for relationships. Small, consistent gestures count for more than grand ones.

Money

Earnings follow activity this year, and so does spending. The equipment, the course, the travel, the round of drinks after a good week — none of it is reckless on its own, and together it can swallow everything the busy year brought in. Give the money a job before it feels like income: a fixed share of anything extra moved out of reach on the day it arrives. Keep the money for whatever you are building separate from the household’s, so you can see plainly whether the project pays for itself.

Steady. A good year to build savings and pay down debt.

Work and study

This is a year to be seen doing the work rather than preparing to do it. The talk, the portfolio, the proposal you normally sit on for another month — put one of them in front of people, because output is what 2027 rewards for a Wood sign. The Tiger trap is starting four things and finishing none; with no external deadline forcing your hand, set your own and tell someone about it. Repetitive maintenance work tends to drain you faster than usual, so trade it away where you can and take the parts that need a first mover.

Keep building what you started. Progress is gradual but real, and it compounds.

Health and energy

It is easy to feel able to keep going well past the sensible point, which is the real risk in a year that burns Wood as fuel. Put the rest in the calendar before you need it — one clear day a week rather than a collapse in August. Fatigue from a loud summer tends to land in the Monkey month (Aug 8 – Sep 7), so keep that stretch lighter than it looks.

No particular weak point this year — keep your routine and it stays that way.

Colors and habits to lean on

A Fire-and-Earth year draws hard on Wood: Fire burns it as fuel and Earth is ground it has to work. Water refills Wood and cools the year, so it is the element to lean on. In the traditional correspondences, Water goes with black and navy, the north and the numbers 1 and 6. Treat them as small reminders rather than charms: your year animal is one character of eight, and the element your own chart needs is worked out from all of them — see the useful god.

What does it mean that the Tiger has no link with the Goat in 2027?
