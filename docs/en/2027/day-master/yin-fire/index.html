Ding Fire in 2027: a Friend year — your own flame returns

2027 · 丁未 · DING WEI · FIRE GOAT

Yin Fire — The Candle Flame · yin Fire
Year stem 丁 = Friend · year branch 未 = Eating God

2027 is a Ding year, and for the Candle Flame that makes the year stem a Friend: your own element, back in the sky. You are more yourself, more visible, and less alone. The Wei ground is your Eating God, so what you make this year comes out easily. Light one thing; light it well.

Overall

Ding Fire is you — the same yin Fire, the same quiet wick — and when the year stem matches the Day Master, the Ten God is Friend. A Friend year is a year of self: identity, independence, allies who are like you, and a strength that comes from company rather than conquest. The Candle Flame usually burns for others in the dark; in 2027 there is a second candle beside it, and the room is suddenly brighter than one flame could manage. Picture a row of lanterns on a summer night, each lit from the next. You will meet people who think like you, join things, start things with a partner, and feel less need to explain yourself. That confidence is the year's gift, and it is also the year's temptation.

Wei is the ground, and for Ding that Earth is the Eating God: your output star — craft, care, cooking, writing, the thing you make with your hands and your attention. Hidden inside the Goat sit Yi Wood (Indirect Resource, the unconventional teacher) and a second Ding (another Friend). Fire feeding Earth is the natural flow of a Flame: you shine, something grows. A strong Ding — summer-born, well fed by Wood — has surplus this year and should spend it on output and on the right allies. A weak Ding — winter-born, thin on Wood, surrounded by Water — is the chart that loves a Friend year most, because the second flame keeps the first alight. Either way, the Ding Wei month in July is your own pillar returning: the year in miniature.

Love

A Friend year pulls you toward people who are like you: same rhythm, same jokes. Singles meet through circles rather than apps — a class, a project, a friend's friend. For coupled Flames the year is warm but crowded: allies, siblings, and colleagues need your attention, and a partner can feel like one more candle in the row. The Eating God ground helps — cooking together, making something together, a child, a pet, a garden — because it gives the warmth an object. Women should note that the Eating God rubs against the Officer partner star: the loyal partner may feel less exciting than the new friend. Notice that, and choose on purpose.

Money

Friend years are not wealth years; they are spending-with-people years — trips, joint ventures, chipping in, picking up the bill. Not bad, but make it deliberate. The Eating God ground is the earning engine: what you make can be sold, and the year favors craft income, teaching, small products, care work. Move in the Wealth months — Wu Shen from 8 August (Direct Wealth in the branch), Ji You from 8 September (Eating God over Indirect Wealth), Geng Xu from 8 October (Direct Wealth stem), Xin Hai from 8 November (Indirect Wealth stem). Avoid buying into a friend's dream in Bing Wu in June, when Rob Wealth stacks on Friend.

Work and study

Independence and alliance at once: a Friend year is the classic time to go out on your own with a partner or stop waiting for permission. The Eating God ground says the work should be something you can point to — a dish, a design, a paper, a patient who got better. The Flame's craftsman temperament is rewarded. The cautions are the Officer months: Ren Yin in February and Ren Zi in December carry Direct Officer on the stem, Gui Mao in March and Gui Chou in January 2028 carry Seven Killings — Water on a candle — and those are months for delivering what you promised, not launching what you dreamed.

Health and energy

The Candle Flame's danger is burnout that gives no warning, and a Friend year hides it well: you feel strong, say yes to everyone, stay for the last round. The Eating God ground adds appetite — good food, late tables, a comfortable weight gain by autumn. Protect the wick with boundaries rather than willpower: a fixed bedtime, a day a week with no plans, meals at a table instead of a desk. Keep an eye on the Water months (Gui Mao in March, Ren Zi in December, Gui Chou in January) for colds and low moods — a candle in the rain — and use them to rest rather than to push.

What a strong Flame must not do is add more candles: crowds, commitments, and shared ventures multiply until the one thing you were good at gets no oxygen. Choose the partner, not the party.

Lean on Jia Chen in April (Direct Resource) and Yi Si in May (Indirect Resource) to prepare, then act in Bing Wu in June and Ding Wei in July, when Fire arrives in force. Keep the Eating God to one craft.

Do and don't in 2027

  • Do — Pick one partner or one collective and build something you can point to by autumn.
  • Do — Turn the craft into income in the autumn Wealth months, August through November.
  • Don't — Do not say yes to every circle; a Friend year multiplies invitations faster than energy.
  • Don't — Do not launch anything big in the Water months of March and December; deliver instead.

FAQ

What Ten God is 2027 for a Yin Fire Day Master?

The year stem 丁 Ding (yin Fire) is your Friend, and the year branch 未 Wei is Eating God through its main hidden stem 己 Ji. That pairing — Friend on top, Eating God underneath — is the shape of the whole year, and the twelve solar months colour it in.

Is 2027 a good year for Ding Fire?

For most Flames, yes. 2027 is a Ding year, so the year stem is a Friend — your own element back in the sky — and Wei Earth is the Eating God, your output star. Weak Ding charts feel stronger and less alone; strong ones get plenty to spend on craft and allies. It is not a wealth year by itself; the money comes from what you make.

What does a Friend year mean?

A Friend year is when the year stem matches your Day Master. It brings people like you — allies, siblings, peers — and a stronger sense of self: more independence, more confidence, less patience with being managed. Weak charts are strengthened; strong charts spend more on people. Good for partnerships and starting things, mixed for money, since Friend competes with Wealth.

When should Ding Fire make money moves in 2027?

Autumn. Wu Shen from 8 August carries Direct Wealth in the branch, Ji You from 8 September puts Eating God over Indirect Wealth (the cleanest earning month), Geng Xu from 8 October has Direct Wealth on the stem, and Xin Hai from 8 November has Indirect Wealth. Use the summer — Bing Wu and Ding Wei — to build, and avoid shared financial ventures in June.
