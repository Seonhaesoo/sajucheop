Overall

Ding Fire controls Metal, and for a Geng Day Master the yin Fire is the Direct Officer: the star of position, responsibility, structure, and being answerable to someone. Classical texts are fond of this pairing — the raw blade needs exactly this kind of steady heat to become a sword, where the fierce Bing Sun would only melt it. So 2027 brings a role, a title, a boss, a deadline, a set of rules, and the experience of being held to them. Picture the smithy: heat, hammer, patience, and a shape emerging that was always inside the iron. Direct Officer years are not comfortable, but they are respectable; people see you as someone who can be trusted with things, and they hand you more of them.

The Wei ground is Earth, and for Geng that Earth is Direct Resource — the star of support, documents, the mother, the mentor who has done this before. Officer on top of Resource is the pattern of promotion by merit: pressure arrives, and something underneath you converts it into standing. Hidden inside the Goat sit Yi Wood (Direct Wealth — an income, or for men a partner) and another Ding (Direct Officer again). A strong Geng — autumn-born, with Earth and Metal in the chart — can take the whole year on the anvil and come out sharper: accept the role, deliver, be measured. A weak Geng — born in spring or summer, thin on Earth — feels the heat more than the shaping and should stay close to the Resource months. Ding Wei in July is the year's own hammer blow.

Love

The Direct Officer is the partner star for women: for a Geng woman, 2027 makes a serious, steady man more likely — someone with a title and a calendar — and an existing partner may ask for more commitment. For men, the hidden Direct Wealth (Yi Wood) inside the Goat is the partner star, quiet but present; the year favors settling over searching, and Yi combines with Geng, so it feels natural. For everyone, the Blade's weakness is tenderness: a year of duty can make you brisk at home. Learn the three soft sentences and use one a day. The Resource ground helps — a partner who feels like shelter, not a project.

Money

Direct Officer years pay in salary and standing rather than windfalls: a raise, a promotion, a fixed-fee contract, a role with an allowance. The Direct Resource ground protects what you earn — documents in order, a settlement, a parent or mentor who helps. The hidden Direct Wealth (Yi Wood) is steady income that tends to stay. Move in the Wealth months: Jia Chen from 5 April (Indirect Wealth over Indirect Resource), Yi Si from 6 May (Direct Wealth stem, Seven Killings beneath — earn, do not gamble), Gui Mao from 6 March (Direct Wealth in the branch). Avoid speculation in Bing Wu in June, when Seven Killings and Direct Officer stack.

Work and study

This is a career year in the old sense: responsibility, rank, and being measured. Take the role even if it is heavier than the pay. Fields with rules and stakes — law, engineering, medicine, uniformed service, operations — notice you in a Direct Officer year. Deliver on time, keep records, and let the Resource ground (a mentor, a certification, a good file) do half the talking. Two cautions: Bing Wu from 6 June, when Seven Killings joins Direct Officer, and the Hurting Officer months — Gui Mao in March, Ren Zi in December, Gui Chou in January — when your own sharp words meet the year's authority. Sheathe the blade there.

Health and energy

Fire on Metal touches the lungs, skin, and breath in traditional terms; at lifestyle level it is a year of pressure — tight shoulders, shallow breathing, a jaw that does not unclench, and pushing through fatigue because someone is counting on you. The Direct Resource ground is your recovery: sleep is not optional in a forging year. Breathe deliberately, walk in cool air, keep the drinking modest, and be careful of overtraining in Bing Wu and Ding Wei, June and July, when the Fire is doubled. Autumn — Wu Shen, Ji You, Geng Xu — is when Metal is in season and the body feels like itself again.

Is 2027 a good year for Geng Metal?

With Resource under it, as in 2027 for Geng, the pressure converts into standing.
