For the Rabbit, 2027 is a harvest. Rabbit and Goat belong to the same trine, and a three-year samjae stretch finally closes — gather in what you planted, and let 2028 be the year for new seeds.

Overall

Your sign is part of the Pig–Rabbit–Goat trine, so the Goat year gathers people around you and widens your circle. It is a year of fruition — and also the last year of the samjae stretch that began in 2025, so harvest and tidy up what you started rather than launching something huge.

For a Wood sign, the year’s Fire is output and its Earth is wealth: you express more, produce more and have more chances to earn — and to spend.

Samjae. In Korean folk tradition, samjae (三災, “three calamities”) is a three-year stretch, once every twelve years, when a sign is advised to take extra care. For the Pig, Rabbit and Goat it runs from 2025 to 2027, and 2027 is the final, “leaving” year (nal-samjae) — traditionally a time to wrap up and consolidate rather than take big risks. It is a custom to be mindful of, not a reason for fear.

Love

Friends introduce friends; a relationship can grow out of a group or a shared cause. Rabbits are affectionate and attentive, but they take time to open up completely. A calm, kind partner earns their full trust.

Money

Money arrives through people — referrals, shared projects, a partner’s network. Close out old accounts before opening new ones.

Work

Team projects shine. Finish what you began in 2025 and collect the credit. Rabbits excel where people and polish meet — design, hospitality, counseling, diplomacy and client work.

Health

Mostly steady. The samjae tradition advises extra care on the road and with minor injuries in the final stretch.

Take more care in: 午 month (Jun 6 – Jul 6), 申 month (Aug 8 – Sep 7), 酉 month (Sep 8 – Oct 7), 子 month (Dec 7 – Jan 5).
