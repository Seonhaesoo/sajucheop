Overall

A good year and a busy one are not the same thing, and 2027 tends to test whether a Rabbit knows the difference. Doors open, invitations multiply, and people bring you into their plans because you are the one who makes a group work. The quiet danger is that an excellent year fills up entirely with other people’s business and leaves your own half-finished. Decide in February — the saju year turns on February 4 — what your harvest actually is: two or three things that must be done by winter, with your name on them.

The year gathers rather than scatters. The Tiger month (Feb 4 – Mar 5), your own month (Mar 6 – Apr 4) and the Dragon month (Apr 5 – May 5) are the stretch for setting your agenda while the year is still young. The Goat month (Jul 7 – Aug 7) and the Pig month (Nov 8 – Dec 6) are when people and results come together, and the Dog month (Oct 8 – Nov 7) is the best of all for agreements. Hold plans loosely in the Rooster month (Sep 8 – Oct 7), and treat this as the year you close things out, leaving new ground for 2028.

Your sign is part of the Pig–Rabbit–Goat trine, so the Goat year gathers people around you and widens your circle. It is a year of fruition — and also the last year of the samjae stretch that began in 2025, so harvest and tidy up what you started rather than launching something huge.

For a Wood sign, the year’s Fire is output and its Earth is wealth: you express more, produce more and have more chances to earn — and to spend.

Samjae. In Korean folk tradition, samjae (三災, “three calamities”) is a three-year stretch, once every twelve years, when a sign is advised to take extra care. For the Pig, Rabbit and Goat it runs from 2025 to 2027, and 2027 is the final, “leaving” year (nal-samjae) — traditionally a time to wrap up and consolidate rather than take big risks. It is a custom to be mindful of, not a reason for fear.

Love

Warmth is easy to find this year; the difficulty is honesty. A Rabbit reads what other people want and quietly supplies it, which is charming at the start of something and corrosive a year in. If you are single, introductions arrive through friends and gatherings rather than chance, and a Dog or a Pig tends to be the easy company. Say early what you actually want, even when it disappoints someone. If you are attached, goodwill runs high enough to carry a real conversation about what comes next — moving in, marrying, or simply agreeing to stay as you are on purpose.

Friends introduce friends; a relationship can grow out of a group or a shared cause.

Money

The Rabbit’s leak is not extravagance, it is the unbilled favor: the design tweaked for free, the weekend given to a friend’s project, the discount offered before anyone asked. In a year when people are asking for your time, put a price on it and say the price out loud. Write terms down even with people you like — especially with people you like. This is a poor year for taking on a long new commitment and a fine one for clearing the last of an old one.

Money arrives through people — referrals, shared projects, a partner’s network. Close out old accounts before opening new ones.

Work and study

Finish, and put your name on it. Rabbits smooth the path, absorb the friction and translate between difficult people, and that work is nearly invisible unless you describe it plainly — so describe it. Anything begun in 2025 or 2026 deserves to be brought to a proper close this year, documented and handed over cleanly rather than carried indefinitely. Qualifications and courses that have been dragging finish well now. Resist the urge to rebuild something from scratch in the last samjae (三災) year, the three-year stretch Korean custom treats with extra care; consolidating pays better.

Team projects shine. Finish what you began in 2025 and collect the credit.

Health and energy

A Rabbit tires from atmosphere, not exertion. Absorbing other people’s moods all week is real work, and this year has a great deal of company in it. Guard a quiet start to the day and one unscheduled stretch each week, and protect them as firmly as a meeting. The autumn run from the Monkey month (Aug 8 – Sep 7) into the Rooster month (Sep 8 – Oct 7) is where a long year of saying yes catches up.

Mostly steady. The samjae tradition advises extra care on the road and with minor injuries in the final stretch.

Colors and habits to lean on

A Fire-and-Earth year draws hard on Wood: Fire burns it as fuel and Earth is ground it has to work. Water refills Wood and cools the year, so it is the element to lean on. In the traditional correspondences, Water goes with black and navy, the north and the numbers 1 and 6. Treat them as small reminders rather than charms: your year animal is one character of eight, and the element your own chart needs is worked out from all of them — see the useful god.
