2027 — the Year of the Fire Goat

丁未 · Ding Wei · 정미
丁未
Yin Fire over the Goat · Feb 6, 2027 – Jan 25, 2028

2027 pairs the stem 丁 — yin fire, the fire of a candle or a lamp — with the branch 未, the Goat, the warm, dry earth of late summer. Fire feeds earth, so the top of the year nourishes the bottom: a gentle, cultivating year that favors care, creativity, home and slow building, with heat and short tempers as its weak spot.

When the Fire Goat year begins

Chinese New Year: Feb 6, 2027 — the Goat year runs to Jan 25, 2028. Korean Seollal: Feb 7, 2027, a day later, because the new moon arrives just before midnight in China and just after it in Korea. Korean saju: the year pillar turns at Ipchun, Feb 4, 2027 at 10:42 Korea time.

Samjae ends for the Pig, Rabbit and Goat

In Korean folk tradition, samjae (三災, “three calamities”) is a three-year stretch, once every twelve years, when a sign is advised to take extra care. For the Pig, Rabbit and Goat it runs from 2025 to 2027, and 2027 is the final, “leaving” year (nal-samjae) — traditionally a time to wrap up and consolidate rather than take big risks. It is a custom to be mindful of, not a reason for fear.
