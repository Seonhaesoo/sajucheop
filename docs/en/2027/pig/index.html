Overall

Pigs are the friends people turn up for, and in 2027 they turn up in numbers: old colleagues call, neighbors invite you in, and a group you belong to suddenly needs you. That company is what gives stalled decisions their momentum. A move you have already saved for, a qualification you are halfway through or a family decision discussed for years has the year behind it. A brand-new venture that needs years of fuel is better planted after samjae, the three-year stretch of care in Korean folk custom, ends in February 2028.

The year gathers early and harvests later. The Tiger month (Feb 4 – Mar 5), the month of your secret-friend sign, and the Rabbit month (Mar 6 – Apr 4), a trine month, are the best time to meet people and set things moving; the Goat month (Jul 7 – Aug 7) is when shared efforts tend to pay off. In between, the Snake month (May 6 – Jun 5), your opposite sign, can unsettle plans, so keep signatures and moves for calmer weeks. Your own Pig month (Nov 8 – Dec 6) is a self-punishment month, a signal to slow down rather than push.

Your sign is part of the Pig–Rabbit–Goat trine, so the Goat year gathers people around you and widens your circle. It is a year of fruition — and also the last year of the samjae stretch that began in 2025, so harvest and tidy up what you started rather than launching something huge.

For a Water sign, the year’s Fire is wealth and its Earth is authority: money opportunities and obligations arrive together — stay organized.

Samjae. In Korean folk tradition, samjae (三災, “three calamities”) is a three-year stretch, once every twelve years, when a sign is advised to take extra care. For the Pig, Rabbit and Goat it runs from 2025 to 2027, and 2027 is the final, “leaving” year (nal-samjae) — traditionally a time to wrap up and consolidate rather than take big risks. It is a custom to be mindful of, not a reason for fear.

Love

A single Pig is most likely to meet someone at a table full of people, and a person you have known for years may suddenly look different. Because you trust quickly and give generously, let a new relationship show you how it handles an ordinary month before you give it everything. For couples, family gatherings multiply and in-laws draw closer, which is warm but tiring; agree in advance how many weekends you can give. A relationship that has drifted along without a name tends to reach a clear answer this year.

Friends introduce friends; a relationship can grow out of a group or a shared cause.

Money

Money in 2027 moves through your social life in both directions. Something you have saved into for years, a shared venture or a group effort can bring in a real harvest; put it toward shrinking debts and strengthening your base before you celebrate. Spending climbs too, so give dinners, gifts and trips a monthly budget and generosity stays a pleasure. Requests for loans and sponsorships multiply for a sign known to say yes, so decide your limit before the first one arrives, and never lend your name to someone else’s loan.

Money arrives through people — referrals, shared projects, a partner’s network. Close out old accounts before opening new ones.

Work and study

At work, the Pig’s 2027 is about finishing well and sharing the credit. Team projects that have run for a year or two tend to reach their payoff, and the people who spread the praise generously are the ones invited onto the next team. If you run a business, partners and suppliers are your strength; tidy those relationships, but hold off on large borrowed expansion until 2028. If you are job-hunting, your network is your best listing, so tell people what you want. Students do well in group projects and presentations.

Team projects shine. Finish what you began in 2025 and collect the credit.

Health and energy

The real risk to your energy is too much of a good thing. Dinners, celebrations and late nights multiply, and three years of accumulated tiredness can surface all at once in the final samjae year. Choose the celebrations that matter and leave the rest early, eat earlier when you can and protect a regular bedtime. Your own Pig month (Nov 8 – Dec 6) is a good moment to lighten the schedule on purpose.

Mostly steady. The samjae tradition advises extra care on the road and with minor injuries in the final stretch.
