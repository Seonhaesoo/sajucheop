Overall

The Snake, Horse and Goat make up the summer trio of the south, so 2027 runs in the same direction you do. Warmth is the word: the year’s Fire matches your own, your colors show clearly, and anything that involves speaking, presenting or promoting goes well. Only the extra heat — impatience and a short fuse — needs managing.

For a Fire sign, the year’s Fire is a peer and its Earth is output: plenty of drive and company, with results people can see. Keep the competitiveness friendly.

Love

A warm, sociable year. Old connections return and new ones start easily. Snakes warm up slowly and then love with great intensity. Once they trust you, they protect the bond fiercely.

Money

Steady rather than spectacular. Money follows visibility, so let people know what you do.

Work

Public-facing work, presentations and marketing are favored. Take the stage when it is offered. Snakes do their best work in analysis, strategy, research, finance and specialist fields, where long preparation pays off.

Health

Too much Fire: watch heat, dehydration, inflammation and sleep.
