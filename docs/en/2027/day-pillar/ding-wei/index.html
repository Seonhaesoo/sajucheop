Overall

2027 is 丁未 — and so are you. Stem and branch both repeat your day pillar, the fullest form of 伏吟, the same note struck twice: Ding is your Friend, 未 Wei is your own branch, and the Eating God you sit on is the year's Eating God too. Nothing foreign arrives; everything familiar gets louder — the warmth, the appetite for making and feeding, the quiet stubbornness, and the low hum of restlessness that comes from living inside a repeat. Ding reaches Wei at Cap and Belt, your own stage as well: dressed, ready, presentable.

An own-pillar year is a mirror. Use it to become more precisely yourself: refine the craft you already have, deepen the relationships you already keep, and let Friend bring the peers who share your taste. Answer the sameness with a project, not an escape. Lean on Gui Mao (from Mar 6), Yi Si (from May 6), Bing Wu (from Jun 6) and Xin Hai (from Nov 8); go gently in Geng Xu (from Oct 8), Ren Zi (from Dec 7) and Gui Chou (from Jan 6, 2028), when the Goat is punished, harmed and finally clashed.

Love

Friend on the stem and your own branch below make 2027 a year of people like you — the same taste, the same tenderness, the same habit of tending others first. A single Ding Wei is likely to meet someone in a shared kitchen, class or studio. A couple may feel the doubled spouse palace as closeness or crowding; the cure is a plan made together. Eating God on both levels loves shared meals — make them the ritual.

Money

Friend years mean shared money, and in an own-pillar year the people closest to you ask: a sibling's plan, a friend's venture, a colleague's split. Keep it written, and kind. Eating God on stem and branch earns through craft — food, teaching, the handmade thing — and Indirect Resource in the Goat rewards a course that raises your rate. Grow the existing income before adding a second; nothing large in the Gui Chou month (from Jan 6, 2028).

Work and study

Food, art, education, counselling — Ding Wei's fields — respond to a year of the same, done better. Cap and Belt says you are ready to be seen; Friend gives you the peers to be seen with. Co-teach, co-author, open the small shared studio, and put your name on the door beside theirs. The trine months, Gui Mao (from Mar 6) and Xin Hai (from Nov 8), are when the proposal, the lease or the launch tends to land.

Home, partner and body

The same branch twice is felt most at home: the same rooms, the same evenings, the same small frictions, slightly amplified. Refresh rather than replace — rearrange, repaint, host — and light one candle a day for yourself, as the pillar's own advice says. Bodily, a Fire pillar in a Fire year runs warm: drink water, sleep before midnight, and keep the Geng Xu month (from Oct 8), when the Dog punishes the Goat, free of renovations and heavy hosting.
