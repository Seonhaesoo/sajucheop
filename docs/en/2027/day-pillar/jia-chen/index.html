Overall

Ding, the 2027 stem, is Jia Chen's Hurting Officer — the star of expression, output and the itch to rewrite the rules. It is Fire drawn from Wood: the tree flowers loudly this year, in ideas, opinions, and the urge to be seen being right. The Goat below carries Direct Wealth, so the flowering can turn into harvest, and Rob Wealth too, so someone will want a share. 未 Wei has no fixed relation to your Dragon, leaving home and body on neutral ground. Jia reaches Wei at Grave: the storehouse, energy kept in rather than shown.

The Tree on Rich Land is generous by nature; a Hurting Officer year tempts it to be generous with words instead of with harvest. Reverse that. Spend the talent on one visible piece of work, keep the opinions for the people who asked, and let Grave do what it does — store. Ren Yin (from Feb 4), Wu Shen (from Aug 8), Ji You (from Sep 8) and Ren Zi (from Dec 7) carry you; take care in Geng Xu (from Oct 8), Xin Hai (from Nov 8) and Gui Chou (from Jan 6, 2028), and treat Gui Mao (from Mar 6) as mixed.

Love

Jia Chen loves through responsibility, and 2027 tests that with a sharper tongue than usual. For a woman, Hurting Officer is the star that argues with the partner star: the correct remark at the wrong moment. For a man, Direct Wealth sits in the Goat — the partner star present and steady, provided the words stay kind. The Dragon and the Goat have no fixed relation, so nothing outside shakes the relationship; only what is said inside it does.

Money

Hurting Officer makes Wealth, and Direct Wealth waits in the Goat: money follows what you produce and show this year — the course you teach, the product you ship, the property you improve. Rob Wealth in the same branch is the partner, sibling or friend who expects a cut; agree the split before the money exists. Grave at Wei favours keeping over expanding: no land or business purchase in the Geng Xu month (from Oct 8), the Dragon–Dog Clash.

Work and study

Business, property, education, running an organisation — Jia Chen's fields — need a Hurting Officer year to be aimed. The talent for the better method is real; announcing it in the meeting is the trap. Write the proposal, attach the numbers, let the result speak. Decline is your own stage and Grave the year's, so delegate rather than pile on. The Wu Shen (from Aug 8) and Ren Zi (from Dec 7) months are your trine months for launches.

Home, partner and body

Chen and Wei have no fixed relation, so the household is level ground this year — steady if you keep it steady, and easy to overlook while the ideas are loud. Book the quiet: a weekly meal with no work talk, and a room the phone does not enter. Grave at Wei means the body recharges slowly; sleep is the investment, and the Xin Hai month (from Nov 8), a wonjin month, is for rest and repairs rather than hosting.
