Overall

For Wu Chen, the Ding stem of 2027 is Direct Resource: warmth from above for a mountain that usually gives shelter and rarely receives it. This is the star of teachers, documents, mothers and quiet learning, and it lands on a Day Master that has always understood more than it says. The Wei branch carries Rob Wealth, so the same year brings peers who want a share, and Wei has no fixed relation with your Chen day branch, leaving home neutral. Wu reaches the Decline stage on Wei: seasoned, unhurried, ripening rather than climbing. The picture is a mountain in warm autumn light with a library at its foot.

Use the year to take in rather than hold up. Study, get the certificate, read the contracts you have been signing on trust, accept help from someone older or wiser. Direct Resource also protects reputation, so this is the year to put your name on the paperwork, the licence, the deed. Let the Rob Wealth branch remind you to keep money and friendship in separate drawers. Act in the Ren Yin month from 4 February, Wu Shen from 8 August, Ji You from 8 September and Ren Zi from 7 December; keep October, the Clash month for Chen, for maintenance.

Love

Direct Resource years make you the one who is looked after; let it happen. Couples benefit if you say the thing you usually keep buried at the foot of the hill; a partner who has felt unread will hear it. For women, Direct Officer hidden in Wei keeps a steady partner star close, favoring upright, reliable people. For men, Rob Wealth in Wei asks you to notice competition early.

Money

Rob Wealth in the Wei branch is the star that shares your purse, so 2027 is for protecting rather than expanding: no guarantees, no informal loans, clear terms with siblings and partners. Direct Resource on top favors income from qualifications, property documents and long-held assets. Spend on learning and on getting the paperwork right; that spending returns. The Ji You month from 8 September, a Six Harmony month for Chen, suits settling accounts and renewing agreements.

Work and study

A study year in a heavy pillar: the mountain that holds others is asked to learn something new, formalize what it knows and let a mentor in. Operations, construction, property and mediation still fit, and a course or certification adds weight to them. The Decline stage says stamina is finite, so delegate the heavy lifting and keep the judgement. The Wu Shen month from 8 August and Ren Zi from 7 December are strong for promotions and contracts.

Home, partner and body

Chen and Wei have no fixed relation, so the household keeps its own rhythm and is not shaken by the year. Direct Resource often shows as a mother, an elder or a helpful neighbour who needs, or offers, attention. Keep the body warm and the routine regular; a Decline year tires the back and the digestion before it tires the will. The Geng Xu month from 8 October clashes with Chen, so avoid moving, big repairs or family confrontations then.
