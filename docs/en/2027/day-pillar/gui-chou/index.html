Overall

Ding, the 2027 stem, is Gui Chou's Indirect Wealth — opportunity, appetite for the world, money in irregular shapes, and for a man the lively form of the partner star. The Goat below carries Seven Killings, the star you already sit on, so pressure and duty come doubled. And 未 Wei meets your Ox in Clash and Punishment: the spouse palace and the ground under you are shaken — a move, a change of routine, a household rearranged. Gui reaches Wei at Grave, the storehouse stage: energy kept inside, best spent slowly.

Do not do both at once. Dew on Frozen Ground endures anything, but enduring a move and a new venture in the same month is how the year becomes heavy. Phase it: settle the ground first, then open the door Indirect Wealth is knocking on, and rest between — resting is also work for this pillar. Act in Yi Si (from May 6), Ji You (from Sep 8), Xin Hai (from Nov 8) and Ren Zi (from Dec 7); take care in Jia Chen (from Apr 5), Bing Wu (from Jun 6), Ding Wei (from Jul 7) and Geng Xu (from Oct 8).

Love

For a man of this pillar, Indirect Wealth on the year stem is the partner star in its sociable form — met through work or travel, lively, not always steady — and the Clash on the spouse palace means the relationship is tested by change: distance, a move, family pressure. For a woman, Seven Killings doubles: the partner star with force. Decide together what stays fixed while everything shifts; lean on each other, do not just endure.

Money

Indirect Wealth years bring money sideways — a commission, a side business — and the appetite to spend it. Under Seven Killings, costs arrive as demands: the deposit, the repair that could not wait. Keep a reserve, store a fixed share on arrival, and let Grave at Wei make you a saver. No property or large loan in the Ding Wei month (from Jul 7), when Clash and Punishment peak, and no speculation in Geng Xu (from Oct 8).

Work and study

Administration, research, medicine, religion — the patient fields where Gui Chou earns trust — meet a year with a real opportunity: a bigger post, a transfer, a public project. Take it with an end date. Seven Killings doubled means pressure from above; Eating God through the Goat means the work will be good, so deliver early. The trine months, Yi Si (from May 6) and Ji You (from Sep 8), are when a move or promotion goes through cleanly.

Home, partner and body

Chou and Wei clash and punish each other, so expect the household to be rearranged — a move, a renovation, a change in who lives where, or routines torn up by a new post. Keep it phased. Grave at Wei means the body recharges slowly: warm food, early nights, a lighter load in the Bing Wu month (from Jun 6), when Harm and wonjin add tiredness, and proper rest before the Ding Wei month (from Jul 7).

Is 2027 good for Gui Chou?

Jia Chen (from Apr 5, Break), Bing Wu (from Jun 6, Harm and wonjin), Ding Wei (from Jul 7, the Ox–Goat Clash and Punishment) and Geng Xu (from Oct 8, Punishment). Avoid big signatures and moves then. Yi Si, Ji You, Xin Hai and Ren Zi are your supportive months.
