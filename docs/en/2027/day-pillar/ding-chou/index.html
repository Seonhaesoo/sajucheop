Overall

For Ding Chou, the Ding stem of 2027 is Friend: a second small flame beside the cauldron, a peer, a fellow craftsman, someone like you. The Wei branch carries Eating God, so the year doubles the urge to make, cook, research and refine. But Wei clashes with and punishes your Chou day branch, so the storehouse that holds your craft is being opened whether you like it or not, and Ding reaches the Cap and Belt stage on Wei, dressed to be seen. The picture is a quiet workshop whose door has been pushed open by a friend and the wind at the same time.

Use the year to bring the work out. A Grave pillar keeps its best pieces inside; a Friend year with Eating God in the branch gives you the companions and the appetite to show them. Let the Chou-Wei clash be a planned move, a new workshop, a stall, a website, rather than a forced one. Act in the Yi Si month from 6 May, Ji You from 8 September, Xin Hai from 8 November and Ren Zi from 7 December, and keep June and July, the Harm and Clash months for Chou, for rest, repairs and quiet accounting. Show the craft and the year rewards it.

Love

Friend on the year stem brings equals into your life, colleagues, fellow makers, people who understand the long hours. Couples feel the Chou-Wei clash as change in the shared routine, a move, new working hours, a partner's restlessness; daily care, your strength, steadies it. For men, Indirect Resource hidden in Wei brings an original, thoughtful person rather than a conventional one. For women, the partner star is quiet, and a relationship grows from shared craft rather than sudden fire.

Money

Friend years share the purse, and Eating God in Wei likes to spend on materials, tools and food, so money leaves through pleasure and companionship rather than through disaster. Earn by selling what you make and by teaching the method; Cap and Belt says put a proper price and a proper label on it. Moves and repairs cost around July, so build a small fund by May, and settle accounts in the Ren Zi month from 7 December.

Work and study

Cooking, craft, research and accounting are your ground, and 2027 is the year to present them: a portfolio, a certification, a first public sale, a team of like-minded people. Cap and Belt on Wei means you are being fitted for a more visible role, so accept it. The Yi Si month from 6 May and Ji You from 8 September support launches and job moves; avoid signing under pressure in the Ding Wei month from 7 July.

Home, partner and body

Chou and Wei sit in Clash and Punishment, so home is where the year moves: a change of address, a renovated kitchen, a partner's new schedule, a parent who needs help. Keep meals and sleep regular, mind the stomach and the back, and in the Ding Wei month from 7 July avoid deciding anything big at the end of a long day. The Geng Xu month from 8 October also brings Punishment, so keep it calm.
