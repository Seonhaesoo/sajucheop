Overall

Ding, the 2027 stem, is Bing Wu's Rob Wealth — a second fire beside the noon sun: the peer, the rival, the sibling, the partner who wants half. For a Yang Ren pillar that already runs hot on competition, the year adds a competitor and raises the temperature. The Goat below carries Hurting Officer, so the heat wants out as words and output. The kindness is in the branch: 未 Wei sits in Six Harmony and the summer trio with your Horse, so home and body take the year well. Bing reaches Wei at Decline — past the summit, and allowed to ease off.

Rob Wealth years are decided by what you refuse to fight over. Choose one arena where competing is worth it and be gracious everywhere else; put shared money on paper; let the rival take the task you did not want anyway. Then use Decline as permission — the Sun at High Noon does not have to prove it is noon. Use Ren Yin (from Feb 4), Yi Si (from May 6), Ding Wei (from Jul 7) and Geng Xu (from Oct 8); take care in Gui Mao (from Mar 6), Ren Zi (from Dec 7) and Gui Chou (from Jan 6, 2028).

Love

Passion is not in short supply; patience is. Rob Wealth stands between a man of this pillar and his partner star this year, so a relationship can be crowded out by friends, work rivals and the need to win. For a woman, Hurting Officer in the Goat sharpens the tongue toward the partner. The Horse–Goat harmony is the good news: home is warm ground, and a partner who feels led rather than commanded stays close. Let them choose the restaurant.

Money

Rob Wealth is the star that shares your money whether you agreed or not: a loan to a friend, a guarantee for a sibling, a handshake venture. Set a ceiling you could lose without a grudge and refuse above it. Hurting Officer in the branch earns through visible skill — competition, performance, sales — so let income come from the arena. No large purchases in the Ren Zi month (from Dec 7), the Rat–Horse Clash.

Work and study

Leadership, sport, business, the front line — Bing Wu's arenas fill with rivals this year, which is where the pillar works best. Compete on the result, not the noise: deliver the complete thing, keep your name on it, share credit before it is taken. Decline at Wei warns against heroic overload; let the second sun carry the second project. The Ren Yin (from Feb 4) and Geng Xu (from Oct 8) months are your trine months for moves and launches.

Home, partner and body

Wu and Wei are in Six Harmony and share the summer trio, so the household is your ally: a base that cools the temper, a partner who sees the person under the noon glare. Leave the competition at the door. Two fires in a Fire month run hot, so take the Ding Wei month (from Jul 7) gently — water, shade, earlier nights — and keep the Gui Chou month (from Jan 6, 2028), a Harm month, for rest.
