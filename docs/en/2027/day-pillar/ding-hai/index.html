Overall

Home, partner and body

Hai and Wei sit in Trine, so home is where 2027 is kindest: a partner who is on your side, a household that hosts, evenings that restore rather than drain. Keep a simple routine for sleep and water so the lighthouse does not burn all night. The Yi Si month from 6 May clashes with Hai, so avoid moving or long trips then, and the Wu Shen month from 8 August asks for tact with relatives.
