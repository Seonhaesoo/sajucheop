Overall

Ding, the 2027 stem, is Yi Mao's Eating God — ease, appetite, creation, the pleasure of making and of children; the gentlest of the output stars, and the one that suits grass best. The Goat below carries Indirect Wealth, opportunity and money in lively shapes, and 未 Wei forms a Wood trine with your Rabbit, so the spouse palace and the household pull together and the grass roots deeper. Yi reaches Wei at Nurture, the stage of being raised: a pillar at its own Official stage has strength to spare, but the year's pace is gentle.

This is one of the kinder years the pillar gets, and its trap is the old one — grass grows in every direction unless it chooses a wall. Choose: one craft to deepen, one opportunity to take, one project with the people at home. Let Eating God do the making and Indirect Wealth the selling. Act in Ren Yin (from Feb 4), Ding Wei (from Jul 7), Geng Xu (from Oct 8) and Xin Hai (from Nov 8); take care in Bing Wu (from Jun 6), Wu Shen (from Aug 8), Ji You (from Sep 8) and Ren Zi (from Dec 7); Jia Chen (from Apr 5) is mixed.

Love

Yi Mao wants a relationship that grows together, and 2027 offers it: the trine on the spouse palace draws a couple into shared plans, and Eating God fills the house with meals, laughter and, for some, a child. For a man, Indirect Wealth in the Goat is the lively partner star — a sociable, bright beginning. For a woman, Eating God can crowd the partner star with output and children; keep evenings for two. Decide when it matters.

Money

Eating God makes money from what you enjoy making, and Indirect Wealth in the Goat turns it into commissions, sales and side income arriving through the trine — through family, friends, the home network. Store a fixed share on arrival; appetite spends as easily as it earns. Friend hides in the same branch, so shared purchases go on paper. Keep large buying out of the Ji You month (from Sep 8), when the Rooster clashes your Rabbit.

Work and study

Education, art, service, counselling — the fields where Yi Mao grows alongside people — respond to an Eating God year with visible output: the course finished, the show mounted, the practice built. Adaptability is the skill; direction is the homework, so name the one thing you are known for by midyear. Nurture at Wei means delegate the grind and keep the craft. The trine months, Ding Wei (from Jul 7) and Xin Hai (from Nov 8), favour launches and moves.

Home, partner and body

Mao and Wei are in trine, so home is the strongest room of the year — a partner and family who back the plan, a house that doubles as a base. Guard the rhythm: Eating God overfills the table and Indirect Wealth the calendar. Regular sleep, real meals and a quiet Wu Shen month (from Aug 8), a wonjin month, keep the body steady; keep the Ren Zi month (from Dec 7) free of renovations.
