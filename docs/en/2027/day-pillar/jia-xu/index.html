Overall

For Jia Xu, the Ding stem of 2027 is Hurting Officer: the tree rooted in dry earth suddenly has a lot to say, sharp ideas, a taste for rule-breaking and a talent for selling. The Wei branch carries Direct Wealth, so the talk can become money, and Rob Wealth hidden there means competitors want a share. Wei meets your Xu day branch in Punishment and Break, though, so the spouse palace and the household feel the strain of a busy year, and Jia reaches the Grave stage on Wei, storing more than it shows. The picture is a tree full of birds while its ground is being dug up.

Use the year to sell and to secure. Hurting Officer with Direct Wealth is a trading combination: pitch, negotiate, market, launch, and finish every job you accept, as this pillar always does. Then spend the saved energy at home, because the Xu-Wei friction shows up as a partner who feels ignored or a house that needs work. Act in the Ren Yin month from 4 February, Gui Mao from 6 March, Bing Wu from 6 June and Wu Shen from 8 August; keep April, the Clash month for Xu, and July, the Punishment and Break month, for maintenance rather than expansion.

Love

Hurting Officer sharpens the tongue, and the Punishment between Xu and Wei tests the spouse palace, so 2027 asks Jia Xu to prove love with attention rather than with provision alone. For men, Direct Wealth in Wei keeps a steady partner star close, and a practical, grounded person suits you. For women, Hurting Officer sits against the partner star, so patience with a partner's pride and routines is the work. Say less, listen longer, and keep July calm.

Money

Direct Wealth in the Wei branch and your own Indirect Wealth realism make this a genuine earning year: sales, property, distribution, deals closed with a persuasive Hurting Officer voice. Rob Wealth hidden in Wei is the leak: partners who overspend, friends who borrow, shared costs that drift. Write the terms down. Put a share of every good month aside for home repairs, which the Xu-Wei Break tends to bring.

Work and study

Business, sales, property and distribution are your field, and a Hurting Officer year gives you the words to enlarge it: marketing, negotiation, teaching what you know. The Grave stage on Wei means the real gains are the ones you store quietly, contracts, savings, a client base, not the ones you announce. The Bing Wu month from 6 June and Wu Shen from 8 August are the strongest windows for launches and new roles.

Home, partner and body

Xu and Wei sit in Punishment and Break, so the household is where the year tests you: a partner who feels second to the business, a renovation that overruns, an elder who needs time. Fix the roof before the rain, keep meals and sleep regular, and watch the stomach and the lower back. The Ding Wei month from 7 July and Gui Chou from 6 January 2028 are the months to stay close to home and off the ladder.
