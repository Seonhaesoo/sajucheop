Overall

Ding, the stem of 2027, is Ding You's Friend — a second candle of exactly your kind, standing beside you. Friend years are about peers: colleagues who share your craft, siblings, rivals who turn out to be allies, and the question of how independent you want to be. The Goat below carries Eating God, so what you make with those peers has warmth and appetite in it. 未 Wei holds no fixed relation to your Rooster, leaving home and body on neutral ground, and Ding reaches Wei at Cap and Belt — dressed for society, ready to be seen.

The Candle Lighting a Gem spends its life making others shine; in a Friend year the peers beside you are as bright as you are, so the old habit of stepping back will simply make you invisible. Take the seat, put your name on the work, and share the stage rather than surrender it. Jia Chen (from Apr 5), Yi Si (from May 6), Wu Shen (from Aug 8) and Gui Chou (from Jan 6, 2028) support you; Ren Yin (from Feb 4), Gui Mao (from Mar 6) and Ren Zi (from Dec 7) call for care, and Geng Xu (from Oct 8) is mixed.

Love

Friend years bring people who resemble you: same taste, same humour, same habit of tending others first. For a single Ding You that is where love is most likely to start — in a shared project or a circle of like minds rather than across a crowded room. For a couple, the risk is a third presence: a friend, a sibling, a colleague who takes up the evenings. Keep the partner's place at the table visibly first.

Money

Friend on the stem means shared money, in the plain sense: joint purchases, split bills, a colleague's venture you are asked to join. Enter nothing whose paperwork you have not read twice. Eating God in the Goat produces income from craft and taste — design, food, beauty, the finished object — which suits you better than speculation. The Ren Zi month (from Dec 7), a Break with your Rooster, is for keeping the wallet closed.

Work and study

Cap and Belt at Wei says you are ready to be seen, and a Friend year gives you the team. Collaborate as an equal: co-author the piece, share the credit line, ask for the title, and offer your eye for finished things where it is visible. Launch in the Yi Si month (from May 6) or the Wu Shen month (from Aug 8); do not quit in the Gui Mao month (from Mar 6), the Rabbit–Rooster Clash.

Home, partner and body

You and Wei have no fixed relation, so the household runs on whatever routine you give it — neither strained by the year nor carried by it. Set that routine deliberately: fixed meals, a fixed bedtime, one evening for the people you live with. Birth is your own stage and Cap and Belt the year's, so energy is decent; spend it on the body's maintenance rather than on hosting every peer who drops by.
