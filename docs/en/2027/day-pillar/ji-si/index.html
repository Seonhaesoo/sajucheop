Overall

For Ji Si, the Ding stem of 2027 is Indirect Resource: unusual insight, a second way of seeing, the teacher who arrives from outside the syllabus. The Wei branch carries Friend, so peers and equals fill the year. Wei joins your Si day branch in a Seasonal trio, the summer alliance of Si, Wu and Wei, which warms home and body, and Ji reaches the Cap and Belt stage on Wei, ready to step into a bigger role. The picture is a field in high summer with a new kind of seed in the ground.

Use the year to learn something sideways. Indirect Resource favors the unconventional: a second discipline, a spiritual or technical study, a mentor who does not think like you. Friend in Wei turns that into a circle, a cohort, a study group. The trap of the year is over-thinking and a touch of isolation, so keep the learning social. Plan the visible moves for the Bing Wu and Ding Wei months from 6 June and 7 July, Ji You from 8 September and Gui Chou from 6 January 2028, and treat November, the Clash month for Si, as a time to stay close to home.

Love

Indirect Resource years are thoughtful rather than passionate, and the Seasonal trio between Si and Wei keeps the spouse palace warm and stable. Couples grow through learning together; a shared course or a shared cause does more than a holiday. For women, Seven Killings hidden in Wei brings a strong, charismatic presence into view, someone who challenges rather than soothes. For men, the partner star is quiet, and Friend in Wei means the best meetings come through peers.

Money

Friend in the Wei branch shares the purse, so joint money needs written terms even between people you trust. Indirect Resource favors spending on knowledge, tools and unusual skills that widen what you can teach or sell later; it does not favor speculation. Income holds steady through your established role, and the Ji You month from 8 September, a Trine for Si, is the best window for negotiating fees or a raise.

Work and study

Education, administration and the professions remain your ground, and 2027 adds a new layer to them: research, a specialist qualification, an approach nobody else in the office has tried. Cap and Belt on Wei means you are being fitted for a more senior seat, so accept responsibility that comes with a title. Share what you know generously; Friend years reward the teacher who is not stingy. Avoid job changes in the Xin Hai month from 8 November.

Home, partner and body

Si and Wei form a Seasonal trio, so the household runs warm and cooperative this year, with relatives and friends dropping in and a partner who pulls in the same direction. Your Prosperity pillar has plenty of drive; the homework is humility and rest, so let the summer months be sociable and the autumn quieter. Watch heat and the heart in July, keep meals regular, and in the Xin Hai month from 8 November avoid long trips and household confrontations.
