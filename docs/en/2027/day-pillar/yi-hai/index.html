Overall

For Yi Hai, the Ding stem of 2027 is Eating God: ease, appetite, creation, the lotus finally opening on the water. The Wei branch carries Indirect Wealth, so the opening attracts opportunity, and Friend is hidden there, so companions gather around it. Wei forms a Trine with your Hai day branch, one of the warmest links a year can give the spouse palace, and Yi reaches the Nurture stage on Wei: being raised, helped, taught. Your Death pillar is quiet and deep by nature; the picture is a lotus that has found its mud and is now visited by bees.

Use the year to make and to root. Eating God wants output, art, teaching, counseling, cooking, and a Trine year gives it an audience and a home to return to. Indirect Wealth in the branch says the output can pay, so put a price on it. The one warning for a floating pillar is dependence; being cared for is the gift of a Nurture year, drifting is its risk. Act in the Gui Mao month from 6 March, Ding Wei from 7 July, Ren Zi from 7 December and Gui Chou from 6 January 2028, and keep May, the Clash month for Hai, for rest and quiet work.

Love

Eating God makes you warm, playful and easy to love, and the Trine between Hai and Wei brings the spouse palace into harmony: couples feel like a team, and singles meet people through creative or convivial settings. For men, Indirect Wealth in Wei brings sociable, generous partners and plenty of attention; choose one lotus pond. For women, the partner star is quiet this year, and the romance is with your own life.

Money

Indirect Wealth in the Wei branch brings money through opportunity, sales and the wider world, and Eating God gives you something to sell: a class, a piece, a service with your taste in it. Friend hidden in Wei shares costs and profits with companions, so keep terms clear even among friends. The Ding Wei month from 7 July is the strongest window for pricing and launching.

Work and study

Art, education, counseling and work abroad are your ground, and 2027 gives them flow and an audience. Eating God years favor work that feels like play. The Nurture stage says accept mentors, elders and helpful documents; The Gui Mao month from 6 March and Ren Zi from 7 December are good for new positions; avoid job changes in the Yi Si month from 6 May.

Home, partner and body

Hai and Wei sit in Trine, so home is where 2027 is kindest: a partner who supports the creative side, a household that hosts, meals that become gatherings. Keep a simple routine for sleep and water so the deep-thinking mind does not float away at night. The Yi Si month from 6 May clashes with Hai, so avoid moving or long trips then, and the Wu Shen month from 8 August asks for tact with relatives.
