Zi and Wei sit in Harm and wonjin — nothing dramatic, just a slow tiredness between people who live together: the sigh, the remark filed away, the small kindness not noticed. Bodily, a Metal Day Master in a Fire year does best with steady sleep and cooler evenings; keep the Ding Wei month (from Jul 7) light on obligations and heavy on rest.
