Overall

For Gui Si, the Ding stem of 2027 is Indirect Wealth: opportunity, appetite for the world, money arriving sideways through people and chances, a lively star for dew that already sits on a seat of wealth, honor and learning. The Wei branch carries Seven Killings, so the opportunity comes with pressure, and Eating God is hidden there as the release. Wei joins your Si day branch in the summer Seasonal trio, warming home and body, and Gui reaches the Grave stage on Wei, collecting, storing, working out of sight. The picture is morning dew at a busy market, sparkling, sought after, and wisest when it gathers into a cup.

Use the year to earn widely and store quietly. Indirect Wealth suits a bright, precise person who notices what people need; sell that noticing in finance, service, education or accounting, and put a firm price on it, because Seven Killings in the branch will push for more than is fair. The Grave stage says the gains that last are the ones you keep. Act in the Bing Wu and Ding Wei months from 6 June and 7 July, Ji You from 8 September and Gui Chou from 6 January 2028, and treat February, October and November as the months to keep your head down.

Love

For men, Indirect Wealth on the year stem is the lively partner star: sociable attention, generous people, more invitations than a Conception pillar usually accepts, on top of the good marriage luck this pillar already carries. For women, Seven Killings in Wei is the partner star, intense and decisive, attractive and a little overwhelming; make sure respect flows both ways. The Seasonal trio between Si and Wei keeps the spouse palace warm, so couples hold steady.

Money

Indirect Wealth years bring irregular money, commissions, sales, a windfall from someone you helped, and Seven Killings in the branch brings sudden costs and hard negotiations. Take the opportunities and store the proceeds; saving beats speaking, and the Grave stage agrees. Do not chase volatile markets, however bright the pitch. Price your thorough work properly and invoice promptly. The Ji You month from 8 September, a Trine for Si, suits settling terms, and October should stay free of new debt.

Work and study

Finance, service, education and accounting stay your ground, and 2027 adds a commercial edge: clients who pay, a supervisor who pushes, a deadline that does not wait. Eating God hidden in Wei is your relief valve; make the work well crafted and kind, and it stops feeling like pressure. The Ji You month from 8 September and Gui Chou from 6 January 2028 support recognition and new roles.

Home, partner and body

Si and Wei form a Seasonal trio, so the household runs warm and cooperative this year: a partner who pulls in the same direction, relatives who visit, a home that absorbs the heat of the office. Keep water and cool evenings against the heat. The Xin Hai month from 8 November clashes with Si, so avoid long trips and household confrontations then.
