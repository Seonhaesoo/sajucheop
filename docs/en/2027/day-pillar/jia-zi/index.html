Overall

For Jia Zi, the Ding Fire stem of 2027 is Hurting Officer: the pine that usually thinks first and moves second suddenly wants to speak, make, publish and argue. Ideas come faster than tact, and that is the whole test of the year. Underneath, the Wei Goat branch carries Direct Wealth, so the output can turn into practical results, but Wei sits in Harm and Wonjin with your Zi day branch, and Jia reaches the Grave stage on Wei. Picture a pine whose branches are loud in the wind while the roots are being asked to go quiet and store.

Use it as a year of expression with an editor. Write, teach, present, build the thing you have been designing in your head, but let every message rest before it goes out. The Grave stage means your strength this year is depth, not volume: fewer projects, finished properly. Lean on the supportive months, Jia Chen from 5 April, Wu Shen from 8 August and the Hai and Chou months at the end of the year, for launches and decisions, and treat June and July as maintenance time. A Hurting Officer year handled with tact leaves a body of work behind.

Love

Hurting Officer years make you witty and a little hard to please. Singles attract attention through conversation; the job is not to critique what you find. For men, Direct Wealth hidden in Wei keeps a steady partner star nearby, and a practical, grounded person suits this year. For women, Hurting Officer sits opposite the partner star, so patience with a partner's routines and authority is the work. Say the kind thing first.

Money

Direct Wealth in the Wei branch rewards earned, steady income: invoices, salary, work priced honestly. Hurting Officer on top tempts you to spend on tools, courses and image, which is fine when it serves an output you will actually finish. Rob Wealth is also hidden in Wei, so keep loans between friends small and written down. Build a buffer during April and August rather than betting on a single big client.

Work and study

The year favors work where understanding becomes something visible: writing, teaching, planning documents, research made public. Jia in the Grave stage on Wei means your best work is done quietly, then released. Friction with managers rises in a Hurting Officer year, so disagree in writing, with a proposed alternative, rather than in the meeting. The Jia Chen month from 5 April and Wu Shen from 8 August are good for pitches and new roles.

Home, partner and body

Zi and Wei sit in Harm and Wonjin, so the household runs on small irritations rather than big storms: schedules that do not match, a partner who feels unheard, a body that wants more sleep than it gets. Keep evening routines steady, decide chores out loud, and stay off sharp words at the dinner table. In the Ding Wei month from 7 July, give the home more attention than the office.
