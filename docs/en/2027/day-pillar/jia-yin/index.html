Work and study

Home, partner and body

Yin and Wei have no fixed relation, so the household is calm ground — easy to overlook while the ideas are loud. Book it in: a weekly meal without work talk, and a room where you are not the leader. Grave at Wei means the body recharges slowly even for a strong pillar; sleep is the investment, and the Yi Si month (from May 6), when Punishment and Harm meet your Tiger, is for rest and routine, not expansion.
