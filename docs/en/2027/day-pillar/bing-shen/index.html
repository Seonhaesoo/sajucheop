Overall

Ding, the 2027 stem, is Bing Shen's Rob Wealth: a second fire in the sky, the peer, the rival, the friend who wants to split the bill and the credit. For a pillar as sociable as the Sun in an Autumn Sky, that means the year fills with people — collaborators, competitors, old contacts resurfacing. The Goat branch has no fixed relation to your Monkey, so home and body stay on an even keel, neither pushed nor pulled. Bing reaches Wei at Decline: past the summit, seasoned, with less appetite for the sprint and more for the well-judged move.

Rob Wealth years go best when you decide early what is shared and what is yours: choose allies, put terms in writing, keep your name on your own work. Then use Decline as it is meant — finish rather than start. Jia Chen (from Apr 5) and Ren Zi (from Dec 7) are your trine months; Ji You (from Sep 8) and Geng Xu (from Oct 8) bring the autumn trio behind you. Handle Ren Yin (from Feb 4), Gui Mao (from Mar 6) and Xin Hai (from Nov 8) with care, and treat Yi Si (from May 6) as mixed.

Love

Charm is not the problem this year; company is. Rob Wealth crowds the calendar and, for a man of this pillar, stands between him and his partner star, so a relationship can starve on attention while the group chat thrives. Book the two-person evenings first. Single Bing Shen meet people through friends and rivals alike; ask early whether someone wants you or your circle. Hurting Officer in the Goat sharpens teasing — soften it.

Money

Rob Wealth is the star that shares your wealth, invited or not. Lending to friends, guaranteeing a sibling, pooling funds with a partner you chose for fun rather than for accounting — these are the 2027 leaks. Decide a ceiling you could lose without resentment and hold it. Hurting Officer in the branch produces money through skill and visibility, so paid speaking, selling and content do well; buy nothing large in the Ren Yin month (from Feb 4).

Work and study

Wide and quick is the Bing Shen style, and this year the field is crowded with people playing the same game. Compete on finishing: the collaborator who delivers the complete version wins the credit Rob Wealth would otherwise split. Partnerships formed in the Jia Chen month (from Apr 5) tend to hold; conflicts with peers surface in the Ren Yin month (from Feb 4), the Tiger–Monkey Clash, and in the Xin Hai month (from Nov 8).

Home, partner and body

Shen and Wei have no fixed relation, so the household is neither the battlefield nor the refuge this year — it takes whatever tone you bring home from the crowd. Bring less of it. Decline at Wei is a hint to trade late nights for early ones, and the Yi Si month (from May 6), when the Snake both harmonises and punishes your Monkey, suits a check of routines rather than a party.
