Overall

For Wu Yin, the Ding stem of 2027 is Direct Resource: shelter, learning, documents, the warmth from above that a mountain used to carrying tigers rarely asks for. Direct Officer is hidden in Wei too, so the year offers proper backing, a senior, a licence, a title. The Wei branch itself carries Rob Wealth, so peers and rivals gather around the same resources, and Wei has no fixed relation with your Yin day branch, leaving home neutral. Wu reaches the Decline stage on Wei: past the summit, seasoned, meant to lead by judgement rather than by charging. The picture is a mountain with a signed deed at its foot.

Use the year to be backed, not to carry. A Birth pillar with Seven Killings below is used to pressure and to being first in a crisis; a Direct Resource year asks it to study, to get the paperwork right and to accept help from above. Let Rob Wealth in the branch teach you to share the load, with clear terms. Act in the Gui Mao and Jia Chen months from 6 March and 5 April, Bing Wu from 6 June and Geng Xu from 8 October, and treat May, August and September as the months to avoid confrontation and keep the plans conservative.

Love

Direct Resource years make you the one who is looked after, which the protector type finds strange and secretly welcome. Let a partner carry something. For women, Direct Officer hidden in Wei keeps a respectable, reliable partner star close, and the year favors commitment with proper introductions. For men, the partner star is quiet, and Rob Wealth in Wei means competition or a friend too close to the relationship; guard it with attention rather than with orders.

Money

Rob Wealth in the Wei branch is the star that shares your purse, so 2027 is for protecting rather than expanding: no guarantees, no loans to admirers, clear splits with partners and siblings. Direct Resource on top favors money through qualifications, property documents and positions held, and spending on learning returns. The Geng Xu month from 8 October, a Trine for Yin, suits contracts and renewals.

Work and study

Leadership, security, business and construction remain your ground, and 2027 asks you to add a credential or a mentor to the authority you already carry. Direct Officer hidden in Wei brings promotion through the proper channel; Direct Resource brings the certificate that makes it stick. The Bing Wu month from 6 June and Geng Xu from 8 October are strong for new posts; avoid power struggles in the Wu Shen month from 8 August.

Home, partner and body

Yin and Wei have no fixed relation, so the household keeps its own rhythm and is not shaken by the year. Direct Resource often shows as a mother, an elder or a helpful neighbour who needs, or offers, attention. Keep the body warm and the routine regular, mind the back and the digestion. The Wu Shen month from 8 August clashes with Yin, so avoid moving, long trips or family showdowns then.
