Overall

Ding, the 2027 stem, is Gui Mao's Indirect Wealth — opportunity, appetite for the world, money that arrives sideways, and for a man the lively form of the partner star. The Goat below carries Seven Killings, so the chance comes with pressure attached: a deadline, a demanding client, a duty. What makes the year kind is the branch: 未 Wei forms a Wood trine with your Rabbit, and Wood is Gui's Eating God, so home and creativity pull together. Gui reaches Wei at Grave, though — the storehouse stage, energy kept inside rather than spent.

Spring Rain's homework is deciding, and an Indirect Wealth year drops several doors in front of you at once. Open one. Let the trine at home be the base you work from, let Eating God make the thing, and let Seven Killings supply the deadline you would never set yourself. Use Ren Yin (from Feb 4), Ding Wei (from Jul 7), Geng Xu (from Oct 8) and Xin Hai (from Nov 8); go carefully in Bing Wu (from Jun 6), Wu Shen (from Aug 8), Ji You (from Sep 8) and Ren Zi (from Dec 7); Jia Chen (from Apr 5) is mixed.

Love

For a man of this pillar, Indirect Wealth on the year stem is the partner star at its most sociable — someone met through work or travel, charming, a little unpredictable. For a woman, Seven Killings sits in the Goat, the partner star with force, arriving through the trine that strengthens your spouse palace. Couples deepen by making something together; home is where the year is warmest. Single Gui Mao should decide, not drift.

Money

Indirect Wealth years bring money in irregular shapes: a commission, a windfall, a side business that suddenly works — and the appetite to spend it. Keep the two separate: a fixed share stored on arrival. Seven Killings in the branch means costs arrive as demands, not choices, so hold a reserve. Grave at Wei favours saving over expanding; do not buy the second thing in the Ji You month (from Sep 8), the Rabbit–Rooster Clash.

Work and study

Art, education, food, service — the fields where Gui Mao raises people — meet a year of chance plus pressure: the bigger client, the public project, the deadline that scares you into finishing. Take one. Eating God through the trine means the work itself will be good; Seven Killings means someone will judge it, so deliver early and cleanly. The Ding Wei (from Jul 7) and Xin Hai (from Nov 8) months are your trine months for pitching or moving.

Home, partner and body

Mao and Wei are in trine, so the household pulls together this year — a partner and family who back the plan, a home that doubles as a workshop or a base. Guard the rhythm: Grave at Wei is a low-battery stage, and Indirect Wealth keeps you out late. Keep sleep regular, eat at home more than out, and use the Wu Shen month (from Aug 8), a wonjin month, for quiet repairs rather than guests.
