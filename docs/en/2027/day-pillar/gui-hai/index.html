Overall

Ding, the 2027 stem, is Gui Hai's Indirect Wealth — opportunity, appetite for the world, money in irregular shapes, and for a man the lively form of the partner star. For the last of the sixty pillars, a deep, intuitive Water at its own Prosperity stage, the year is an invitation to come ashore. The Goat below carries Seven Killings, so the chance has a deadline attached, and Eating God, so the making is good. 未 Wei forms a Wood trine with your Pig: home and creativity pull together. Gui reaches Wei at Grave, the storehouse — energy kept in, best spent slowly.

Reality was always this pillar's homework, and an Indirect Wealth year hands it a real-world door: a client, a sale, a project with a public face. Open one, let the trine at home be the harbour you work from, and let Seven Killings supply the deadline intuition never sets. Use Gui Mao (from Mar 6), Ding Wei (from Jul 7), Ren Zi (from Dec 7) and Gui Chou (from Jan 6, 2028); take care in Jia Chen (from Apr 5), Yi Si (from May 6) and Wu Shen (from Aug 8); Ren Yin (from Feb 4) is mixed.

Love

For a man, Indirect Wealth on the year stem is the partner star at its most sociable — met through travel or work, charming and hard to predict. For a woman, Seven Killings sits in the Goat, the partner star with force, arriving through the trine on your spouse palace. Gui Hai loves deep and free; this year the depth is easy and the practical part — plans, dates, a shared address — is the romance. Decide, then stay.

Money

Indirect Wealth years bring money sideways — a commission, a sale, a side venture — and the appetite to spend it. Store a fixed share on arrival; Grave at Wei favours the saver. Seven Killings in the Goat means costs arrive as demands, so keep a reserve. The trine brings opportunity through people close to home; take it with terms in writing. Keep speculation out of the Yi Si month (from May 6), the Snake–Pig Clash.

Work and study

Research, art, work abroad, counselling — the fields where Gui Hai's eye for currents earns — get a real opportunity with a real deadline: the bigger client, the public project, the transfer overseas. Take one, with an end date. Eating God through the Goat means the work will be good; Seven Killings means it will be judged, so deliver early. The trine months, Gui Mao (from Mar 6) and Ding Wei (from Jul 7), favour pitches and moves.

Home, partner and body

Hai and Wei are in trine, so the household is the year's harbour — a partner and family behind the plan, a home that doubles as a base. Guard the rhythm: Grave at Wei is a low-battery stage, and Indirect Wealth keeps you out late. Regular sleep, real meals, and a quiet Wu Shen month (from Aug 8), a Harm month, for repairs, not guests; keep the Jia Chen month (from Apr 5), a wonjin month, free of big decisions.
