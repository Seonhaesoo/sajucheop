Overall

For Ren Chen, the Ding stem of 2027 is Direct Wealth: steady, practical harvest, money that comes from work done properly, and for men the marriage star. Wei has no fixed relation with your Chen day branch, leaving home neutral, and Ren reaches only the Nurture stage on Wei, being raised rather than ruling. The picture is a reservoir filling slowly from a reliable stream, with the dragon asked to wait one more season before it rises.

Use the year to fill, not to release. The dragon in the reservoir wants its moment; 2027 gives it steady income, an official role and time to gather, which is the harder gift. Accept the structure Direct Officer brings, keep the position that pays, and let a mentor train you; the Nurture stage rewards the leader who can be an apprentice for a season. Direct Wealth on the stem and Direct Officer in the branch make 2027 a harvest through structure: salary, fees, contracts renewed. Home, partner and body

Chen and Wei have no fixed relation, so the household keeps its own weather and is not shaken by the year. Keep the body moving and the water flowing; a Grave pillar in a Nurture year stores tension in the back and the kidneys. The Geng Xu month from 8 October clashes with Chen, so avoid moving, big repairs and family confrontations then.
