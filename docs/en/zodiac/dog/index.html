Personality

Strengths: loyalty; a strong sense of fairness; responsibility.

Challenges: anxiety and constant vigilance; rigidity; distrust that is slow to fade.

In love. Dogs are devoted, constant partners. Doubt makes them anxious, so honesty matters to them more than romance.

At work. Dogs are the backbone of an organization: law, public service, healthcare, security and teaching — wherever integrity counts.

The five Dogs

The year’s heavenly stem adds one of five elements, so a 1984 Dog and a 1996 one are not quite the same animal.

  • Wood Dog1934, 1994
    The Wood Dog is warm and cooperative, the Dog most at ease in a team.
  • Fire Dog1946, 2006
    The Fire Dog is passionate and outspoken, a natural advocate for people and causes.
  • Earth Dog1958, 2018
    The Earth Dog is steady and responsible, slow to change its mind and slower still to abandon anyone.
  • Metal Dog1970, 2030
    The Metal Dog is the most principled and determined, with a strict sense of right and wrong.
  • Water Dog1982
    The Water Dog is the gentlest Dog: understanding, perceptive and more flexible than the rest.
