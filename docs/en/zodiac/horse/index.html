Personality

Strengths: energy and enthusiasm; quick decisions; warmth that lifts a group.

Challenges: restlessness; moods that swing with circumstances; losing interest before the finish.

In love. Horses fall fast and passionately. If a relationship starts to feel like a fence, their heart leaves first.

At work. Horses come alive in sales, travel, fieldwork, events and anything that moves. Desk-bound routine wears them out.

The five Horses

The year’s heavenly stem adds one of five elements, so a 1984 Horse and a 1996 one are not quite the same animal.

  • Wood Horse1954, 2014
    The Wood Horse is friendly and cooperative, the Horse most able to channel its energy into a shared goal.
  • Fire Horse1966, 2026
    The Fire Horse is the most famous and the most fiery: passionate, daring and impossible to ignore.
  • Earth Horse1978
    The Earth Horse is the steadiest Horse — still quick, but sensible about money and commitments.
  • Metal Horse1930, 1990
    The Metal Horse is independent and headstrong, with great stamina and a strong need for freedom.
  • Water Horse1942, 2002
    The Water Horse is adaptable and witty, a skilled communicator who changes course with ease.

Element associations

The Horse’s branch carries Fire (火) — warmth, drive and visibility. In Korean tradition, Fire goes with red and orange, the south, and the numbers 2 and 7.
