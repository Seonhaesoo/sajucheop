Born in January or early February 1993?

The 1993 Rooster year began at Chinese New Year on Jan 23, 1993. If you were born from January 1 to Jan 22, 1993, your sign is the Water Monkey of 1992. The year ended on Feb 9, 1994, so birthdays in early 1994 up to that date still count as Roosters.

The Korean saju year starts at Ipchun

In Korean saju (the Four Pillars), the year pillar changes at Ipchun, the start of spring — Feb 4, 1993 at 04:37 Korea time — not at Lunar New Year. A birthday from Jan 23 to Feb 3, 1993 is already a Rooster by Lunar New Year but still 壬申 (Water Monkey) in a saju chart.

The Water Rooster personality
