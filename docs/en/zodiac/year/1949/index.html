Born in January or early February 1949?

The 1949 Ox year began at Chinese New Year on Jan 29, 1949. If you were born from January 1 to Jan 28, 1949, your sign is the Earth Rat of 1948. The year ended on Feb 16, 1950, so birthdays in early 1950 up to that date still count as Oxs.

The Korean saju year starts at Ipchun

In Korean saju (the Four Pillars), the year pillar changes at Ipchun, the start of spring — Feb 4, 1949 at 12:30 Korea time — not at Lunar New Year. A birthday from Jan 29 to Feb 3, 1949 is already a Ox by Lunar New Year but still 戊子 (Earth Rat) in a saju chart.

The Earth Ox personality

The Earth Ox is immovable in the best sense — dependable, frugal and content with slow progress that lasts. Both the year stem and the Ox’s own branch are Earth, so the Earth character is doubled this year. Earth brings steadiness, patience and practical sense.

Oxen trust actions over words and finishing over speed. Once they decide, they keep going long after others have stopped. Strengths: reliability people can build on; patience with slow, hard work; calm under pressure. Watch for: stubbornness once a view is set; slowness to adapt; difficulty putting feelings into words.
