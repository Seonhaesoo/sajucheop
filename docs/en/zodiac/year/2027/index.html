Born in January or early February 2027?

The Korean saju year starts at Ipchun

In Korean saju (the Four Pillars), the year pillar changes at Ipchun, the start of spring — Feb 4, 2027 at 10:42 Korea time — not at Lunar New Year. A birthday from Feb 4 to Feb 5, 2027 is already 丁未 (Fire Goat) in a saju chart but still a Horse by Lunar New Year. In Korea, Seollal (Lunar New Year) fell on Feb 7, 2027, one day after the Chinese date, because the new moon arrived just before midnight in China and just after it in Korea.
