Born in January or early February 2026?

The 2026 Horse year began at Chinese New Year on Feb 17, 2026. If you were born from January 1 to Feb 16, 2026, your sign is the Wood Snake of 2025. The year ended on Feb 5, 2027, so birthdays in early 2027 up to that date still count as Horses.

The Korean saju year starts at Ipchun

In Korean saju (the Four Pillars), the year pillar changes at Ipchun, the start of spring — Feb 4, 2026 at 05:02 Korea time — not at Lunar New Year. The Fire Horse personality

The Fire Horse is the most famous and the most fiery: passionate, daring and impossible to ignore. Both the year stem and the Horse’s own branch are Fire, so the Fire character is doubled this year. Fire brings warmth, drive and visibility.

Horses are bright, active and fast. They hate being tied down and are always half a step toward the next new thing. Strengths: energy and enthusiasm; quick decisions; warmth that lifts a group. Watch for: restlessness; moods that swing with circumstances; losing interest before the finish.

Questions about the 2026 zodiac

What Chinese zodiac animal is 2026?

2026 is the Year of the Horse — the Fire Horse (丙午). The Horse year runs from Feb 17, 2026 to Feb 5, 2027: it starts at Chinese New Year, not on January 1.

Is someone born in January 2026 a Horse?

Only if the birthday falls on or after Feb 17, 2026, when the Horse year began. From January 1 to Feb 16, 2026, the sign is still the Wood Snake of 2025.

What element is the 2026 Horse?

Fire. The year stem 丙 is yang fire, so everyone born in the 2026 Horse year is a Fire Horse. Fire brings warmth, drive and visibility.

When is the next Year of the Horse?

2038 — the Earth Horse. The Fire Horse itself comes back every sixty years — 1966 and 2086.

Who is the Horse most compatible with?

The Horse’s best matches are the Goat (96/100), the Tiger (84/100) and the Dog (84/100); the hardest are the Rat (32) and the Ox (45).
