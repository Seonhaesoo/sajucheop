Born in January or early February 1962?

The 1962 Tiger year began at Chinese New Year on Feb 5, 1962. If you were born from January 1 to Feb 4, 1962, your sign is the Metal Ox of 1961. The year ended on Jan 24, 1963, so birthdays in early 1963 up to that date still count as Tigers.

The Korean saju year starts at Ipchun

In Korean saju (the Four Pillars), the year pillar changes at Ipchun, the start of spring — Feb 4, 1962 at 16:14 Korea time — not at Lunar New Year. A birthday from Feb 4, 1962 is already 壬寅 (Water Tiger) in a saju chart but still a Ox by Lunar New Year.

The Water Tiger personality

The Water Tiger is the calmest Tiger: perceptive, open-minded and able to wait for the right moment. Water brings intuition, adaptability and depth.

Tigers see the big picture and move first. They are generous and exciting to be around, and sometimes leave the clean-up to someone else. Strengths: the courage to start what others only discuss; natural authority; protectiveness toward the people they lead. Watch for: impatience; pride that turns small conflicts into big ones; boredom with routine.
