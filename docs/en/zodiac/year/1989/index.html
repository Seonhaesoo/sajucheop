Born in January or early February 1989?

The 1989 Snake year began at Chinese New Year on Feb 6, 1989. If you were born from January 1 to Feb 5, 1989, your sign is the Earth Dragon of 1988. The year ended on Jan 26, 1990, so birthdays in early 1990 up to that date still count as Snakes.

The Korean saju year starts at Ipchun

In Korean saju (the Four Pillars), the year pillar changes at Ipchun, the start of spring — Feb 4, 1989 at 05:23 Korea time — not at Lunar New Year. A birthday from Feb 4 to Feb 5, 1989 is already 己巳 (Earth Snake) in a saju chart but still a Dragon by Lunar New Year.

The Earth Snake personality
