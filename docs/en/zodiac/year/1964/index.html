Born in January or early February 1964?

The 1964 Dragon year began at Chinese New Year on Feb 13, 1964. If you were born from January 1 to Feb 12, 1964, your sign is the Water Rabbit of 1963. The year ended on Feb 1, 1965, so birthdays in early 1965 up to that date still count as Dragons.

The Korean saju year starts at Ipchun

In Korean saju (the Four Pillars), the year pillar changes at Ipchun, the start of spring — Feb 5, 1964 at 03:56 Korea time — not at Lunar New Year. A birthday from Feb 5 to Feb 12, 1964 is already 甲辰 (Wood Dragon) in a saju chart but still a Rabbit by Lunar New Year.

The Wood Dragon personality

The Wood Dragon is creative and expansive, a Dragon who wants to build something that outlives them. Wood brings growth, generosity and a long view.

Dragons think on a large scale and live in a world of their own design. They are at their most powerful when they feel recognized. Strengths: vision and ambition; confidence that inspires others; generosity once they commit. Watch for: pride that finds it hard to back down; impatience with details; sensitivity to criticism.
