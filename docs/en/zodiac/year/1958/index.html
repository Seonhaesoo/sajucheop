Born in January or early February 1958?

The 1958 Dog year began at Chinese New Year on Feb 18, 1958. If you were born from January 1 to Feb 17, 1958, your sign is the Fire Rooster of 1957. The year ended on Feb 7, 1959, so birthdays in early 1959 up to that date still count as Dogs.

The Korean saju year starts at Ipchun

In Korean saju (the Four Pillars), the year pillar changes at Ipchun, the start of spring — Feb 4, 1958 at 16:48 Korea time — not at Lunar New Year. A birthday from Feb 4 to Feb 17, 1958 is already 戊戌 (Earth Dog) in a saju chart but still a Rooster by Lunar New Year. In Korea, Seollal (Lunar New Year) fell on Feb 19, 1958, one day after the Chinese date, because the new moon arrived just before midnight in China and just after it in Korea.

The Earth Dog personality

The Earth Dog is steady and responsible, slow to change its mind and slower still to abandon anyone. Both the year stem and the Dog’s own branch are Earth, so the Earth character is doubled this year. Earth brings steadiness, patience and practical sense.

Dogs are honest and dependable. Once you are on their side, they defend you to the end, and they cannot stand injustice. Strengths: loyalty; a strong sense of fairness; responsibility. Watch for: anxiety and constant vigilance; rigidity; distrust that is slow to fade.
