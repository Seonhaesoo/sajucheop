Born in January or early February 1946?

The 1946 Dog year began at Chinese New Year on Feb 2, 1946. If you were born from January 1 to Feb 1, 1946, your sign is the Wood Rooster of 1945. The year ended on Jan 21, 1947, so birthdays in early 1947 up to that date still count as Dogs.

The Korean saju year starts at Ipchun

In Korean saju (the Four Pillars), the year pillar changes at Ipchun, the start of spring — Feb 4, 1946 at about 19:05 Korea time — not at Lunar New Year. A birthday from Feb 2 to Feb 3, 1946 is already a Dog by Lunar New Year but still 乙酉 (Wood Rooster) in a saju chart. Why saju starts the year at Ipchun →

Questions about the 1946 zodiac

What Chinese zodiac animal is 1946?

1946 is the Year of the Dog — the Fire Dog (丙戌). The Dog year runs from Feb 2, 1946 to Jan 21, 1947: it starts at Chinese New Year, not on January 1.

Is someone born in January 1946 a Dog?

Only if the birthday falls on or after Feb 2, 1946, when the Dog year began. From January 1 to Feb 1, 1946, the sign is still the Wood Rooster of 1945.

What element is the 1946 Dog?

Fire. The year stem 丙 is yang fire, so everyone born in the 1946 Dog year is a Fire Dog. Fire brings warmth, drive and visibility.

When is the next Year of the Dog?

2030, starting Feb 3, 2030 — the Metal Dog. The Fire Dog itself comes back every sixty years — 2006.

Who is the Dog most compatible with?

The Dog’s best matches are the Horse (84/100), the Rabbit (76/100) and the Monkey (74/100); the hardest are the Dragon (41) and the Goat (44).
