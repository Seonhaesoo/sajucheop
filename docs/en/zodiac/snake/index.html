Personality

Strengths: sharp analysis; the patience to prepare properly; composure and quiet charisma.

Challenges: suspicion; holding on to hurt for a long time; secrecy that others misread.

In love. Snakes warm up slowly and then love with great intensity. Once they trust you, they protect the bond fiercely.

At work. Snakes do their best work in analysis, strategy, research, finance and specialist fields, where long preparation pays off.

The five Snakes

The year’s heavenly stem adds one of five elements, so a 1984 Snake and a 1996 one are not quite the same animal.

  • Wood Snake1965, 2025
    The Wood Snake is thoughtful and cultured, the most patient Snake and the most willing to share.
  • Fire Snake1977
    The Fire Snake is charismatic and ambitious, energetic in pursuit and quick to see through people.
  • Earth Snake1929, 1989
    The Earth Snake is calm and methodical, the most down-to-earth and dependable of the Snakes.
  • Metal Snake1941, 2001
    The Metal Snake is the most determined: self-reliant, discerning and very hard to deceive.
  • Water Snake1953, 2013
    The Water Snake is intuitive and wise, reading undercurrents that others never notice.
