The two signs

Rabbit — the gentle diplomatRabbits read the mood and put people at ease. They avoid open conflict, but under the softness is a firm sense of what they will and won’t accept.
Pig — the generous optimistPigs are frank, kind-hearted and trusting. They give freely, and people gather around them because of it.

How the branches meet — Natural allies

The Rabbit (卯) and the Pig (亥) belong to the Wood trine of Pig, Rabbit and Goat. Point this pair at the same goal and their strength multiplies, which is why they do so well building a business, a project or a household together. They match because they face the same direction, not because they are alike. Keep a shared aim in view; without one, things can drift.

The elements

The Pig’s Water feeds the Rabbit’s Wood, as rain grows a tree. The Pig looks after and the Rabbit grows; it lasts when the Rabbit says so out loud and the Pig remembers to look after themselves too.

In love and marriage

Love deepens when there is a plan or a passion you share. A goal-driven couple. Rabbits are affectionate and attentive, but they take time to open up completely. A calm, kind partner earns their full trust. Pigs love sincerely and accept partners as they are. They are happiest with someone as straightforward as they are.

As friends and colleagues

Excellent partners at work — on the same team you deliver; as rivals you exhaust each other. Rabbits excel where people and polish meet — design, hospitality, counseling, diplomacy and client work. Pigs do well with people and in teams — hospitality, healthcare, charity and sales — and quietly build wealth through consistency.

As family

A real strength when the family takes on something big together: a move, a business, exams. The Rabbit’s weak spot is difficulty saying no; the Pig’s is difficulty saying no. Knowing that solves half the problem.

Making it work

  • Write down one goal the two of you share
  • Match your pace so one of you is not always out in front
  • Share the credit when it works
