How the branches meet — Secret friends

The Dragon (辰) and the Rooster (酉) form one of the six harmony pairs, the branches that recognize each other as partners. Conversation comes easily and the relationship feels older than it is. When one of you wobbles, the other steadies things, so love and business partnerships both start smoothly. The one risk is taking each other for granted — say thank you out loud and this bond lasts.

The elements

The Dragon’s Earth feeds the Rooster’s Metal, as earth holds the ore. The Dragon gives and the Rooster grows, so the Dragon becomes the steady support — and the balance depends on the Rooster giving thanks in return.

In love and marriage

Attraction comes quickly and arguments blow over. Tradition counts it among the best signs for marriage. Dragons find it hard to bend first, but when they give their heart, they give it grandly. Roosters can be blunt enough to hurt, but few people take responsibility for a relationship as seriously.

As friends and colleagues

Friends and colleagues who understand each other without explanation. Split the roles and you work like one team. Dragons belong on big stages and big projects — leadership, entrepreneurship, performance and public life. Small, fiddly tasks bore them. Roosters earn trust in roles that need accuracy — management, accounting, quality control, medicine and law.

As family

As parent and child, a close and supportive bond — just keep expectations realistic. The Dragon’s weak spot is pride that finds it hard to back down; the Rooster’s is criticism that stings. Knowing that solves half the problem.

Making it work

  • Keep comfort from turning into indifference — say what you appreciate
  • Take turns making the big decisions
  • Tackle big things together and let each other handle the small ones
