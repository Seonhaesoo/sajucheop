How the branches meet — Opposites

The Dragon (辰) and the Dog (戌) sit directly opposite each other on the zodiac wheel. There can be a strong pull at first, but your methods run in opposite directions, so collisions are frequent: when one moves, the other stops; when one talks, the other shuts down. This is less a bad bond than a different one. Stop trying to change each other, turn the difference into a division of labor, and you can do together what neither could alone.

The elements

Both are Earth signs, so you see the world in similar ways. Understanding comes quickly, but you may compete for the same ground — divide your territory and you become firm allies.

In love and marriage

Strong chemistry, big arguments over small things. If you marry, agree on the house rules early. Dragons find it hard to bend first, but when they give their heart, they give it grandly. Dogs are devoted, constant partners. Doubt makes them anxious, so honesty matters to them more than romance.

As friends and colleagues

Better at a little distance. You work best in different roles rather than side by side. Dragons belong on big stages and big projects — leadership, entrepreneurship, performance and public life. Small, fiddly tasks bore them. Dogs are the backbone of an organization: law, public service, healthcare, security and teaching — wherever integrity counts.

As family

Between parent and child, friction tends to peak in the teenage years; space works better than control. The Dragon’s weak spot is pride that finds it hard to back down; the Dog’s is anxiety and constant vigilance. Knowing that solves half the problem.

Making it work

  • Never settle an argument on the day it starts
  • Put rules about money, time and chores in writing
  • Stop trying to fix the other person’s way of doing things
