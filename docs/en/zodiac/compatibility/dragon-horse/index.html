The two signs

Dragon — the big-hearted visionaryDragons think on a large scale and live in a world of their own design. They are at their most powerful when they feel recognized.
Horse — the free-spirited doerHorses are bright, active and fast. They hate being tied down and are always half a step toward the next new thing.

How the branches meet — A clean slate

The Dragon (辰) and the Horse (午) have neither a harmony nor a clash between their branches. Nothing pulls you together and nothing pushes you apart, so the temperature of the relationship is up to the two of you. In pairings like this, the elements and your individual characters matter more. It suits people who get to know each other slowly.

The elements

The Horse’s Fire feeds the Dragon’s Earth, as fire enriches the soil with ash. The Horse looks after and the Dragon grows; it lasts when the Dragon says so out loud and the Horse remembers to look after themselves too.

In love and marriage

The kind of match that shows its worth on the second or third date, not the first. Dragons find it hard to bend first, but when they give their heart, they give it grandly. Horses fall fast and passionately. If a relationship starts to feel like a fence, their heart leaves first.

As friends and colleagues

Starts as a pleasant acquaintance and grows into a long friendship. Dragons belong on big stages and big projects — leadership, entrepreneurship, performance and public life. Small, fiddly tasks bore them. Horses come alive in sales, travel, fieldwork, events and anything that moves. Desk-bound routine wears them out.

As family

An easygoing relationship, warmer still when the elements nourish each other. The Dragon’s weak spot is pride that finds it hard to back down; the Horse’s is restlessness. Knowing that solves half the problem.

Making it work

  • Look at what your elements add to each other
  • Agree on expectations early
  • Find one interest you share
