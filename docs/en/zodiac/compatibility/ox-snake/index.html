How the branches meet — Natural allies

The Ox (丑) and the Snake (巳) belong to the Metal trine of Snake, Rooster and Ox. Point this pair at the same goal and their strength multiplies, which is why they do so well building a business, a project or a household together. They match because they face the same direction, not because they are alike. Keep a shared aim in view; without one, things can drift.

The elements

The Snake’s Fire feeds the Ox’s Earth, as fire enriches the soil with ash. The Snake looks after and the Ox grows; it lasts when the Ox says so out loud and the Snake remembers to look after themselves too.

In love and marriage

Love deepens when there is a plan or a passion you share. A goal-driven couple. An Ox rarely makes grand declarations; they love by staying — showing up the same way, year after year. Snakes warm up slowly and then love with great intensity. Once they trust you, they protect the bond fiercely.

As friends and colleagues

Excellent partners at work — on the same team you deliver; as rivals you exhaust each other. Give an Ox a clear responsibility and they will carry it to the end. They shine in operations, engineering, farming, finance and any role where trust is the product. Snakes do their best work in analysis, strategy, research, finance and specialist fields, where long preparation pays off.

As family

A real strength when the family takes on something big together: a move, a business, exams. The Ox’s weak spot is stubbornness once a view is set; the Snake’s is suspicion. Knowing that solves half the problem.

Making it work

  • Write down one goal the two of you share
  • Match your pace so one of you is not always out in front
  • Share the credit when it works
