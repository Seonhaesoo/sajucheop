How the branches meet — A clean slate

The Ox (丑) and the Rabbit (卯) have neither a harmony nor a clash between their branches. Nothing pulls you together and nothing pushes you apart, so the temperature of the relationship is up to the two of you. In pairings like this, the elements and your individual characters matter more. It suits people who get to know each other slowly.

The elements

The Rabbit’s Wood controls the Ox’s Earth, as roots break through soil. The Rabbit tends to lead while the Ox adapts; balance comes when the Rabbit eases off and the Ox speaks up. The tension also tempers you both.

In love and marriage

The kind of match that shows its worth on the second or third date, not the first. An Ox rarely makes grand declarations; they love by staying — showing up the same way, year after year. Rabbits are affectionate and attentive, but they take time to open up completely. A calm, kind partner earns their full trust.

As friends and colleagues

Starts as a pleasant acquaintance and grows into a long friendship. Give an Ox a clear responsibility and they will carry it to the end. They shine in operations, engineering, farming, finance and any role where trust is the product. Rabbits excel where people and polish meet — design, hospitality, counseling, diplomacy and client work.

As family

An easygoing relationship, warmer still when the elements nourish each other. The Ox’s weak spot is stubbornness once a view is set; the Rabbit’s is difficulty saying no. Knowing that solves half the problem.

Making it work

  • Look at what your elements add to each other
  • Agree on expectations early
  • Find one interest you share

Does it matter who is the man and who is the woman?
