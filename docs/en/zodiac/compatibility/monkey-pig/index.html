Pig — the generous optimistPigs are frank, kind-hearted and trusting. They give freely, and people gather around them because of it.

How the branches meet — Hidden strain

The Monkey (申) and the Pig (亥) meet in a harm (hai) relationship: it looks fine on the surface while small losses and hurts accumulate underneath. One person’s kindness can feel like a burden to the other, and help can come back as resentment. Lower your expectations and be clear about what is given and taken, and it can last a long time.

The elements

The Monkey’s Metal feeds the Pig’s Water, as a spring rises from rock. The Monkey gives and the Pig grows, so the Monkey becomes the steady support — and the balance depends on the Pig giving thanks in return.

In love and marriage

Keep devotion from turning into a debt — ask for what you want in words. Monkeys win people over with wit and cannot stand boredom. In serious moments they may deflect with a joke. Pigs love sincerely and accept partners as they are. They are happiest with someone as straightforward as they are.

As friends and colleagues

Money and favors are where this goes wrong. Keep personal loans out of it. Ideas and improvisation are the Monkey’s tools: technology, trading, marketing, entertainment — anything with many moving parts. Pigs do well with people and in teams — hospitality, healthcare, charity and sales — and quietly build wealth through consistency.

As family

Trying to look after each other can leave both exhausted; a step back is kinder. The Monkey’s weak spot is restlessness; the Pig’s is difficulty saying no. Knowing that solves half the problem.

Making it work

  • Ask before doing someone a favor
  • Decide it is fine not to get back as much as you give
  • Handle money as clearly as you would with a stranger
