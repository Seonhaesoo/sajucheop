How the branches meet — A clean slate

Nothing pulls you together and nothing pushes you apart, so the temperature of the relationship is up to the two of you. In pairings like this, the elements and your individual characters matter more. It suits people who get to know each other slowly.

The elements

The Goat’s Earth feeds the Monkey’s Metal, as earth holds the ore. The Goat gives and the Monkey grows, so the Goat becomes the steady support — and the balance depends on the Monkey giving thanks in return.

In love and marriage

The kind of match that shows its worth on the second or third date, not the first. Goats are devoted and romantic, and they treasure atmosphere. When insecure, they need more reassurance than they will ask for. Monkeys win people over with wit and cannot stand boredom. In serious moments they may deflect with a joke.

As friends and colleagues

Starts as a pleasant acquaintance and grows into a long friendship. Goats shine in creative, caring and service work — the arts, design, education and healthcare. Cut-throat competition drains them. Ideas and improvisation are the Monkey’s tools: technology, trading, marketing, entertainment — anything with many moving parts.

As family

An easygoing relationship, warmer still when the elements nourish each other. The Goat’s weak spot is worry; the Monkey’s is restlessness. Knowing that solves half the problem.

Making it work

  • Agree on expectations early
  • Find one interest you share
