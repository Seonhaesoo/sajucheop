Personality

Strengths: reliability people can build on; patience with slow, hard work; calm under pressure.

Challenges: stubbornness once a view is set; slowness to adapt; difficulty putting feelings into words.

In love. An Ox rarely makes grand declarations; they love by staying — showing up the same way, year after year.

At work. Give an Ox a clear responsibility and they will carry it to the end. They shine in operations, engineering, farming, finance and any role where trust is the product.

The five Oxs

The year’s heavenly stem adds one of five elements, so a 1984 Ox and a 1996 one are not quite the same animal.

  • Wood Ox1925, 1985
    The Wood Ox is the most flexible Ox — still steady, but willing to listen and to work through people rather than around them.
  • Fire Ox1937, 1997
    The Fire Ox has a leader’s drive under the calm surface: proud, principled and quietly competitive.
  • Earth Ox1949, 2009
    The Earth Ox is immovable in the best sense — dependable, frugal and content with slow progress that lasts.
  • Metal Ox1961, 2021
    The Metal Ox is determined and exact, a perfectionist who holds themselves to a harder standard than anyone else.
  • Water Ox1973
    The Water Ox is the most thoughtful Ox: patient, observant and more emotionally attuned than they let on.

Element associations

The Ox’s branch carries Earth (土) — steadiness, patience and practical sense. In Korean tradition, Earth goes with yellow and beige, the the center — close to home, and the numbers 5 and 10.
