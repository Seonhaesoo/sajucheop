The cycle runs Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, Pig. Each animal is one of the twelve earthly branches; each year also carries one of ten heavenly stems, which gives it an element — Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal or Water — and the two together repeat every sixty years. 2026 is the Fire Horse; 2027 is the Fire Goat.
