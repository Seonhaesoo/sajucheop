Personality

Strengths: creativity and taste; compassion; a gift for making people feel cared for.

Challenges: worry; hesitation over big decisions; leaning too heavily on others when anxious.

In love. Goats are devoted and romantic, and they treasure atmosphere. When insecure, they need more reassurance than they will ask for.

At work. Goats shine in creative, caring and service work — the arts, design, education and healthcare. Cut-throat competition drains them.

The five Goats

The year’s heavenly stem adds one of five elements, so a 1991 Goat and a 2003 one are not quite the same animal.

  • Wood Goat1955, 2015
    The Wood Goat is warm and generous, the Goat most at ease in groups and communities.
  • Fire Goat1967, 2027
    The Fire Goat is braver and more expressive than other Goats, with a strong creative streak around a sensitive core.
  • Earth Goat1979
    The Earth Goat is the most self-reliant Goat: practical, caring and good at making a comfortable home.
  • Metal Goat1931, 1991
    The Metal Goat has an artist’s eye and an inner resilience that surprises people who took them for soft.
  • Water Goat1943, 2003
    The Water Goat is the most sensitive: intuitive, gentle and happiest where they feel understood.

Element associations

The Goat’s branch carries Earth (土) — steadiness, patience and practical sense. In Korean tradition, Earth goes with yellow and beige, the center (close to home), and the numbers 5 and 10.

Beyond the year animal

Your animal sign is one of eight characters in a Korean saju chart, and not the one that describes you most closely. That job belongs to the Day Master, the stem of your birth day: ten natures from the Tall Pine to the Morning Dew. What saju is explains how the eight fit together.

Questions about the Goat

What years are the Year of the Goat?

1931, 1943, 1955, 1967, 1979, 1991, 2003, 2015 and 2027. Each Goat year starts at Chinese New Year, between January 21 and February 20, so a birthday early in one of those years can still belong to the sign before.

When is the next Year of the Goat?

2027, from Feb 6, 2027 to Jan 25, 2028 — the Fire Goat. The most recent was 2015, the Wood Goat.

What is the Goat most compatible with?

The Goat’s best matches are the Horse (96/100), the Snake (74/100) and the Rabbit (72/100); the hardest are the Ox (29) and the Rat (33).

What element is the Goat?

The Goat’s branch 未 carries Earth, which it keeps every year. The year’s stem adds a second element, so there are five kinds of Goat: Wood Goat, Fire Goat, Earth Goat, Metal Goat, Water Goat.
