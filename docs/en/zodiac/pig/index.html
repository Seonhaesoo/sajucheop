Personality

Strengths: generosity; sincerity; patience and steady effort.

Challenges: difficulty saying no; trusting too easily; indulgence.

In love. Pigs love sincerely and accept partners as they are. They are happiest with someone as straightforward as they are.

At work. Pigs do well with people and in teams — hospitality, healthcare, charity and sales — and quietly build wealth through consistency.

The five Pigs

The year’s heavenly stem adds one of five elements, so a 1984 Pig and a 1996 one are not quite the same animal.

  • Wood Pig1935, 1995
    The Wood Pig is kind and persuasive, the Pig best at rallying people around a good cause.
  • Fire Pig1947, 2007
    The Fire Pig is warm and adventurous, generous with time and money and quick to enjoy life.
  • Earth Pig1959, 2019
    The Earth Pig is patient and practical, the Pig most likely to turn steady effort into security.
  • Metal Pig1971, 2031
    The Metal Pig is sociable yet determined, with firmer opinions than most Pigs.
  • Water Pig1983
    The Water Pig is intuitive, compassionate and sincere to the core.
