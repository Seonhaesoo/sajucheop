Personality

Strengths: quick thinking; versatility; humor that disarms people.

Challenges: restlessness; being taken as unserious; difficulty sticking to one path.

In love. Monkeys win people over with wit and cannot stand boredom. In serious moments they may deflect with a joke.

At work. Ideas and improvisation are the Monkey’s tools: technology, trading, marketing, entertainment — anything with many moving parts.

The five Monkeys

The year’s heavenly stem adds one of five elements, so a 1984 Monkey and a 1996 one are not quite the same animal.

  • Wood Monkey1944, 2004
    The Wood Monkey is inventive and sociable, the Monkey most interested in improving things for everyone.
  • Fire Monkey1956, 2016
    The Fire Monkey is energetic and competitive, full of ideas and not shy about taking the lead.
  • Earth Monkey1968, 2028
    The Earth Monkey is the most reliable Monkey — clever but steady, with genuine concern for others.
  • Metal Monkey1980
    The Metal Monkey is sharp, confident and very self-sufficient, with a strong competitive streak.
  • Water Monkey1932, 1992
    The Water Monkey is perceptive and flexible, strategic enough to get their way without a fight.
