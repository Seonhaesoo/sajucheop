Personality

Strengths: vision and ambition; confidence that inspires others; generosity once they commit.

Challenges: pride that finds it hard to back down; impatience with details; sensitivity to criticism.

In love. Dragons find it hard to bend first, but when they give their heart, they give it grandly.

At work. Dragons belong on big stages and big projects — leadership, entrepreneurship, performance and public life. Small, fiddly tasks bore them.

The five Dragons

The year’s heavenly stem adds one of five elements, so a 1984 Dragon and a 1996 one are not quite the same animal.

  • Wood Dragon1964, 2024
    The Wood Dragon is creative and expansive, a Dragon who wants to build something that outlives them.
  • Fire Dragon1976
    The Fire Dragon is the most intense: magnetic, outspoken and driven by strong convictions.
  • Earth Dragon1928, 1988
    The Earth Dragon is steady and strategic, more patient and more practical than other Dragons.
  • Metal Dragon1940, 2000
    The Metal Dragon is strong-willed and uncompromising, the hardest of all to talk out of a plan.
  • Water Dragon1952, 2012
    The Water Dragon is the most adaptable, pairing ambition with diplomacy and a good sense of timing.

Element associations
