Lunar months in May 2026

  • The 3rd month of 2026, the Year of the Fire Horse: April 17, 2026 – May 16, 2026 (30 days)
  • The 4th month of 2026, the Year of the Fire Horse: May 17, 2026 – June 14, 2026 (29 days)

Festivals

Buddha’s Birthday (佛誕) — Sunday, May 24, 2026. The 8th day of the 4th month. Temples hang lotus lanterns for the birth of the Buddha. It is a public holiday in South Korea, Hong Kong and Macau.

Solar terms

Start of Summer (立夏, Lixia) — Tuesday, May 5, 2026, 19:49 Beijing time. Summer begins in the calendar, weeks before it feels like it. In a saju chart the Snake month opens: the season of Fire.

Grain Buds (小滿, Xiaoman) — Thursday, May 21, 2026, 08:37 Beijing time. “Small fullness”: the grain fills but has not ripened. Barley turns golden and everything is still growing.

Four Pillars months in May 2026

A saju or BaZi chart does not follow the lunar months above. Its month changes at a solar term: until Start of Summer on May 5 the month pillar is 壬辰 (Ren Chen), and from that moment it is 癸巳 (Gui Si). Solar terms of 2026, with Korea times · Cast a chart

FAQ

What is the lunar date on May 1, 2026?

Friday, May 1, 2026 is the 15th day of the 3rd lunar month of the Chinese year 2026 (Fire Horse). The day pillar is 乙亥 (Yi Hai).

When does a new lunar month start in May 2026?

The 4th lunar month starts on Sunday, May 17, 2026. A lunar month begins on the day of the new moon, counted in Beijing time.

When is the Buddha’s Birthday in 2026?

Sunday, May 24, 2026, the 8th day of the 4th month.

Which zodiac year is May 2026 in?

All of May 2026 falls in the Year of the Fire Horse, which runs from February 17, 2026 to February 5, 2027.
