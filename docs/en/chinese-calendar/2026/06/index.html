Lunar months in June 2026

  • The 4th month of 2026, the Year of the Fire Horse: May 17, 2026 – June 14, 2026 (29 days)
  • The 5th month of 2026, the Year of the Fire Horse: June 15, 2026 – July 13, 2026 (29 days)

Festivals

Dragon Boat Festival (端午節) — Friday, June 19, 2026. The 5th day of the 5th month. Dragon-boat races and zongzi, sticky rice wrapped in leaves, in memory of the poet Qu Yuan. Korea’s Dano falls on the same lunar date with customs of its own, the Gangneung Danoje festival among them. It is a public holiday in mainland China.

Solar terms

Grain in Ear (芒種, Mangzhong) — Friday, June 5, 2026, 23:48 Beijing time. Time to sow the bearded grains and transplant rice. The Horse month opens, the most yang month of the year.

Summer Solstice (夏至, Xiazhi) — Sunday, June 21, 2026, 16:25 Beijing time. The longest day and shortest night. The peak of light is also the turning point: from here the nights lengthen.

Four Pillars months in June 2026
