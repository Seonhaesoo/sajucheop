Chinese calendar, February 2026

February 2026 opens on the 14th day of the 12th lunar month and closes on the 12th day of the 1st lunar month: it starts in the Year of the Wood Snake and ends in the Year of the Fire Horse. The 1st lunar month begins on February 17. Festivals this month: Little New Year on February 10, New Year’s Eve on February 16 and Spring Festival on February 17.

Lunar months in February 2026

  • The 12th month of 2025, the Year of the Wood Snake: January 19, 2026 – February 16, 2026 (29 days)
  • The 1st month of 2026, the Year of the Fire Horse: February 17, 2026 – March 18, 2026 (30 days)

Festivals

Little New Year (小年) — Tuesday, February 10, 2026. The 23rd day of the 12th month. The Kitchen God leaves to make his yearly report, and the house is cleaned for New Year. Northern China keeps it on the 23rd of the 12th month, the south on the 24th.

New Year’s Eve (除夕) — Monday, February 16, 2026. The last day of the 12th month (29th). Chuxi, the last day of the lunar year: the reunion dinner, staying up past midnight, and fireworks where they are allowed.

Spring Festival (春節) — Tuesday, February 17, 2026. The 1st day of the 1st month. Chinese New Year, the first day of the first lunar month. Families gather the night before for the reunion dinner, children receive red envelopes, and the celebrations run until the Lantern Festival on the 15th. It is a public holiday in mainland China.

Solar terms

Start of Spring (立春, Lichun) — Wednesday, February 4, 2026, 04:02 Beijing time. The saju New Year. At this moment the year pillar and the month pillar change together, and so does the zodiac animal in a chart. Korean households paste 立春大吉 on the door.

Rain Water (雨水, Yushui) — Wednesday, February 18, 2026, 23:52 Beijing time. Snow turns to rain and the ice begins to melt. What was frozen, in the fields and in plans, starts to loosen.

Four Pillars months in February 2026

A saju or BaZi chart does not follow the lunar months above. Its month changes at a solar term: until Start of Spring on February 4 the month pillar is 己丑 (Ji Chou), and from that moment it is 庚寅 (Geng Yin). The year pillar turns at the same moment, from 乙巳 (Wood Snake) to 丙午 (Fire Horse): in a chart, the new year starts here and not at Lunar New Year. FAQ

What is the lunar date on February 1, 2026?

Sunday, February 1, 2026 is the 14th day of the 12th lunar month of the Chinese year 2025 (Wood Snake). The day pillar is 丙午 (Bing Wu).

When does a new lunar month start in February 2026?

The 1st lunar month starts on Tuesday, February 17, 2026. A lunar month begins on the day of the new moon, counted in Beijing time.

When is the Little New Year in 2026?

Tuesday, February 10, 2026, the 23rd day of the 12th month.

Which zodiac year is February 2026 in?

The Year of the Wood Snake ends on February 16, 2026, and the Year of the Fire Horse begins on February 17, 2026, Chinese New Year.
