Festivals

Mid-Autumn Festival (中秋節) — Friday, September 25, 2026. The 15th day of the 8th month. Mooncakes and moon-viewing on the full moon of the eighth month. In Korea the same date is Chuseok, a three-day harvest holiday and the biggest family gathering of the year. It is a public holiday in mainland China.

Solar terms

White Dew (白露, Bailu) — Monday, September 7, 2026, 22:41 Beijing time. Dew forms as the nights cool. The Rooster month opens, the sharpest month of Metal.

Autumn Equinox (秋分, Qiufen) — Wednesday, September 23, 2026, 08:05 Beijing time. Day and night are equal again, and from here the nights are longer. A time for taking stock.
