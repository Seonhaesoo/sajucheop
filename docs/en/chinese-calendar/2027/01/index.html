Lunar months in January 2027

  • The 11th month of 2026, the Year of the Fire Horse: December 9, 2026 – January 7, 2027 (30 days)
  • The 12th month of 2026, the Year of the Fire Horse: January 8, 2027 – February 5, 2027 (29 days)

Festivals

Laba Festival (臘八節) — Friday, January 15, 2027. The 8th day of the 12th month. A bowl of Laba congee, cooked from mixed grains, beans and dried fruit, opens the run-up to New Year.

Little New Year (小年) — Saturday, January 30, 2027. The 23rd day of the 12th month. The Kitchen God leaves to make his yearly report, and the house is cleaned for New Year. Northern China keeps it on the 23rd of the 12th month, the south on the 24th.

Solar terms

Minor Cold (小寒, Xiaohan) — Tuesday, January 5, 2027, 22:10 Beijing time. Despite the name, usually the coldest stretch of the Korean year. The Ox month opens: the last month of the saju year, frozen ground where plans are made.

Major Cold (大寒, Dahan) — Wednesday, January 20, 2027, 15:30 Beijing time. The last of the twenty-four. “Great cold” by name, but spring is close: the saju year ends at the next term.

Four Pillars months in January 2027
