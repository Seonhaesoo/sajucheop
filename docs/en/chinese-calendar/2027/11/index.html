Solar terms

Start of Winter (立冬, Lidong) — Sunday, November 7, 2027, 23:39 Beijing time. Winter begins; in Korea it is kimchi-making (김장) season. The Pig month opens: the season of Water.

Minor Snow (小雪, Xiaoxue) — Monday, November 22, 2027, 21:16 Beijing time. The first light snow, and the ground begins to freeze. Preparations for winter are finished.

FAQ

What is the lunar date on November 1, 2027?

Monday, November 1, 2027 is the 4th day of the 10th lunar month of the Chinese year 2027 (Fire Goat). The day pillar is 甲申 (Jia Shen).

When does a new lunar month start in November 2027?

The 11th lunar month starts on Sunday, November 28, 2027. A lunar month begins on the day of the new moon, counted in Beijing time.

Which zodiac year is November 2027 in?

All of November 2027 falls in the Year of the Fire Goat, which runs from February 6, 2027 to January 25, 2028.
