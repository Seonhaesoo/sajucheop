Chinese calendar, August 2027

August 2027 opens on the 29th day of the 6th lunar month and closes on the 30th day of the 7th lunar month, in the Chinese year 2027, the Year of the Fire Goat. The 7th lunar month begins on August 2. Festivals this month: Qixi Festival on August 8 and Ghost Festival on August 16.

Lunar months in August 2027

  • The 6th month of 2027, the Year of the Fire Goat: July 4, 2027 – August 1, 2027 (29 days)
  • The 7th month of 2027, the Year of the Fire Goat: August 2, 2027 – August 31, 2027 (30 days)

Festivals

Qixi Festival (七夕) — Sunday, August 8, 2027. The 7th day of the 7th month. The one night of the year when the Cowherd and the Weaver Girl meet across the Milky Way, now often called Chinese Valentine’s Day. Korea knows it as Chilseok; Japan’s Tanabata comes from the same story.

Ghost Festival (中元節) — Monday, August 16, 2027. The 15th day of the 7th month. Zhongyuan, the middle of Ghost Month, when families make offerings to ancestors and to wandering spirits. The Korean name for the day is Baekjung.

Solar terms

Start of Autumn (立秋, Liqiu) — Sunday, August 8, 2027, 01:27 Beijing time. Autumn begins in the calendar while the heat lingers. The Monkey month opens: the season of Metal, of harvest and finishing.

End of Heat (處暑, Chushu) — Monday, August 23, 2027, 16:14 Beijing time. The heat withdraws; mornings and evenings turn cool. Koreans say the mosquitoes’ mouths go crooked at Cheoseo.

Four Pillars months in August 2027

FAQ

What is the lunar date on August 1, 2027?

Sunday, August 1, 2027 is the 29th day of the 6th lunar month of the Chinese year 2027 (Fire Goat). The day pillar is 壬子 (Ren Zi).

When does a new lunar month start in August 2027?

The 7th lunar month starts on Monday, August 2, 2027. A lunar month begins on the day of the new moon, counted in Beijing time.

When is the Qixi Festival in 2027?

Sunday, August 8, 2027, the 7th day of the 7th month.

Which zodiac year is August 2027 in?

All of August 2027 falls in the Year of the Fire Goat, which runs from February 6, 2027 to January 25, 2028.
