Lunar months in June 2027

  • The 4th month of 2027, the Year of the Fire Goat: May 6, 2027 – June 4, 2027 (30 days)
  • The 5th month of 2027, the Year of the Fire Goat: June 5, 2027 – July 3, 2027 (29 days)

Festivals

Dragon Boat Festival (端午節) — Wednesday, June 9, 2027. The 5th day of the 5th month. Dragon-boat races and zongzi, sticky rice wrapped in leaves, in memory of the poet Qu Yuan. Korea’s Dano falls on the same lunar date with customs of its own, the Gangneung Danoje festival among them. It is a public holiday in mainland China.

Solar terms

Grain in Ear (芒種, Mangzhong) — Sunday, June 6, 2027, 05:26 Beijing time. Time to sow the bearded grains and transplant rice. The Horse month opens, the most yang month of the year.

Summer Solstice (夏至, Xiazhi) — Monday, June 21, 2027, 22:11 Beijing time. The longest day and shortest night. The peak of light is also the turning point: from here the nights lengthen.

Four Pillars months in June 2027

FAQ

What is the lunar date on June 1, 2027?

Tuesday, June 1, 2027 is the 27th day of the 4th lunar month of the Chinese year 2027 (Fire Goat). The day pillar is 辛亥 (Xin Hai).

When does a new lunar month start in June 2027?

The 5th lunar month starts on Saturday, June 5, 2027. A lunar month begins on the day of the new moon, counted in Beijing time.

When is the Dragon Boat Festival in 2027?

Wednesday, June 9, 2027, the 5th day of the 5th month.

Which zodiac year is June 2027 in?
