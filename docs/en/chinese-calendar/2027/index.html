Chinese and Korean calendars in 2027

The two calendars follow the same rules in different time zones, Beijing (UTC+8) and Seoul (UTC+9), so a new moon in the last hour before midnight in Beijing starts the month a day later in Korea. In 2027 that happens once:

  • The 1st lunar month (New Year): February 6, 2027 in China, February 7, 2027 in Korea.

FAQ

When is Chinese New Year 2027?

Saturday, February 6, 2027. It opens the Year of the Fire Goat, which lasts until January 25, 2028.

Is there a leap month in 2027?

No. The Chinese year 2027 has twelve months and 354 days. The next leap month is a leap 5th month in 2028.

When is the Mid-Autumn Festival in 2027?

Wednesday, September 15, 2027, the 15th day of the 8th lunar month. Korea’s Chuseok is the same day.

When is the Dragon Boat Festival in 2027?

Wednesday, June 9, 2027, the 5th day of the 5th lunar month.

Why do some websites show a date one day off?

Usually a time zone. The Chinese calendar is counted in Beijing time, Korea’s in Seoul time and Vietnam’s in Hanoi time, and a new moon close to midnight lands on different dates. A few converters also use simplified tables rather than the astronomical rules.
