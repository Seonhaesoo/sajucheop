Solar terms

Minor Heat (小暑, Xiaoshu) — Wednesday, July 7, 2027, 15:37 Beijing time. The real heat begins and the rainy season peaks. The Goat month opens, the dry Earth of late summer.

Major Heat (大暑, Dashu) — Friday, July 23, 2027, 09:05 Beijing time. The hottest stretch of the year, when the monsoon ends and the heat settles in.

FAQ

What is the lunar date on July 1, 2027?

Thursday, July 1, 2027 is the 27th day of the 5th lunar month of the Chinese year 2027 (Fire Goat). The day pillar is 辛巳 (Xin Si).

When does a new lunar month start in July 2027?

The 6th lunar month starts on Sunday, July 4, 2027. A lunar month begins on the day of the new moon, counted in Beijing time.

Which zodiac year is July 2027 in?

All of July 2027 falls in the Year of the Fire Goat, which runs from February 6, 2027 to January 25, 2028.
