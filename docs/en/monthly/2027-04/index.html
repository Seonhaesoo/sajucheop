April 2027
甲辰
Yang Wood over the Dragon · Monday, April 5, 2027 09:17 – Wednesday, May 5, 2027 (KST)

The Dragon month is spring’s damp Earth, the storehouse of Water. It is a month of foundations and paperwork, of deals reaching their middle rather than their end, and of a certain restlessness as spring turns toward summer. Yang Wood on top gives the month a straightforward, upward push: projects start and plans get stated plainly.

Saju counts months from one solar term to the next, so this month begins at 09:17 on Apr 5 and ends on May 5. Each sign below is read two ways: how the Dragon branch meets the sign’s own branch, and how the month’s Wood meets the sign’s element. The best and care days are the days in the month whose day pillar harmonizes or clashes with the sign, computed from the sixty-day cycle.

For the ten Day Masters

Your animal sign is one character of eight. The stem of the month, Yang Wood, meets each Day Master as one of the Ten Gods, and that relationship sets the month’s theme more precisely than the animal does. Don’t know your Day Master? Your birth date is enough.

  • Yang Wood (The Tall Pine) — Friend: a month of standing on your own — peers gather and independence works; keep money matters separate.
  • Yin Wood (The Winding Vine) — Rob Wealth: a competitive month — drive rises and so does shared spending; avoid lending.
  • Yang Fire (The Midday Sun) — Indirect Resource: deep, unusual insight — study and research pull you in; don’t postpone the one decision that matters.
  • Yin Fire (The Candle Flame) — Direct Resource: support and green lights — approvals, mentors and documents; ask for help and it tends to arrive.
  • Yang Earth (The Great Mountain) — Seven Killings: pressure and proving — demands land on you; meet them squarely and protect your rest.
  • Yin Earth (The Fertile Field) — Direct Officer: structure favors you — applications, reviews and formal steps go well if you follow the rules.
  • Yang Metal (The Raw Blade) — Indirect Wealth: opportunity and spending both swell — decide the budget before the month decides it for you.
  • Yin Metal (The Polished Gem) — Direct Wealth: steady gains — routine work pays, promises hold, and it is a fair month for sensible signatures.
  • Yang Water (The Open Sea) — Eating God: a month of ease and output — make, cook, write, show, and enjoy without overdoing it.
  • Yin Water (The Morning Dew) — Hurting Officer: a month of sharp expression — good for pitching and persuading, careful around authority.

FAQ

When does the Dragon month of 2027 begin and end?

It runs from Monday, April 5, 2027 at 09:17 Korea time, the solar term that opens it, to Wednesday, May 5, 2027; the next month begins on May 6 at 02:25. Saju months follow the solar terms, not the calendar, which is why the Dragon month straddles April and May.

Which zodiac signs have the best April 2027?

the Rat, the Tiger, the Rabbit, the Monkey and the Rooster — their branches form a harmony with the Dragon. The Ox, the Dragon, the Dog and the Pig meet more friction and should take the month steadily. The rest have no fixed link and read the month by its element, Wood.

What is the month pillar for April 2027?

甲辰 — Jia Chen, Yang Wood over the Dragon. The stem, Yang Wood, sets the month’s tone; the branch, the Dragon, decides how each sign meets it. It sits inside the 丁未 year.

Is a monthly horoscope by zodiac sign the same as a saju reading?

No. Your animal sign is one of eight characters in a saju chart, so this page reads one character against the month. A personal reading starts from your Day Master, the stem of your birth day; cast your chart and today’s reading compares each day with all of it.
