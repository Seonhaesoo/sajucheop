丙午
Yang Fire over the Horse · Sunday, June 6, 2027 06:26 – Tuesday, July 6, 2027 (KST)

The Horse month is the peak of Fire, and the summer solstice sits inside it. Energy, exposure and tempers all run high. Things get decided quickly and publicly. Book the rest before the month starts, not after. Yang Fire on top puts everything in daylight: visibility, warmth, and no hiding place.

Saju counts months from one solar term to the next, so this month begins at 06:26 on Jun 6 and ends on Jul 6. Each sign below is read two ways: how the Horse branch meets the sign’s own branch, and how the month’s Fire meets the sign’s element. The best and care days are the days in the month whose day pillar harmonizes or clashes with the sign, computed from the sixty-day cycle.

For the ten Day Masters

Your animal sign is one character of eight. The stem of the month, Yang Fire, meets each Day Master as one of the Ten Gods, and that relationship sets the month’s theme more precisely than the animal does. Don’t know your Day Master? Your birth date is enough.

  • Yang Wood (The Tall Pine) — Eating God: a month of ease and output — make, cook, write, show, and enjoy without overdoing it.
  • Yin Wood (The Winding Vine) — Hurting Officer: a month of sharp expression — good for pitching and persuading, careful around authority.
  • Yang Fire (The Midday Sun) — Friend: a month of standing on your own — peers gather and independence works; keep money matters separate.
  • Yin Fire (The Candle Flame) — Rob Wealth: a competitive month — drive rises and so does shared spending; avoid lending.
  • Yang Earth (The Great Mountain) — Indirect Resource: deep, unusual insight — study and research pull you in; don’t postpone the one decision that matters.
  • Yin Earth (The Fertile Field) — Direct Resource: support and green lights — approvals, mentors and documents; ask for help and it tends to arrive.
  • Yang Metal (The Raw Blade) — Seven Killings: pressure and proving — demands land on you; meet them squarely and protect your rest.
  • Yin Metal (The Polished Gem) — Direct Officer: structure favors you — applications, reviews and formal steps go well if you follow the rules.
  • Yang Water (The Open Sea) — Indirect Wealth: opportunity and spending both swell — decide the budget before the month decides it for you.
  • Yin Water (The Morning Dew) — Direct Wealth: steady gains — routine work pays, promises hold, and it is a fair month for sensible signatures.

FAQ

When does the Horse month of 2027 begin and end?

It runs from Sunday, June 6, 2027 at 06:26 Korea time, the solar term that opens it, to Tuesday, July 6, 2027; the next month begins on Jul 7 at 16:37. Saju months follow the solar terms, not the calendar, which is why the Horse month straddles June and July.

Which zodiac signs have the best June 2027?

the Tiger, the Snake, the Goat and the Dog — their branches form a harmony with the Horse. The Rat, the Ox, the Rabbit and the Horse meet more friction and should take the month steadily. The rest have no fixed link and read the month by its element, Fire.

What is the month pillar for June 2027?

丙午 — Bing Wu, Yang Fire over the Horse. The stem, Yang Fire, sets the month’s tone; the branch, the Horse, decides how each sign meets it. It sits inside the 丁未 year.

Is a monthly horoscope by zodiac sign the same as a saju reading?

No. Your animal sign is one of eight characters in a saju chart, so this page reads one character against the month. A personal reading starts from your Day Master, the stem of your birth day; cast your chart and today’s reading compares each day with all of it.
