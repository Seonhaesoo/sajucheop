December 2027
壬子
Yang Water over the Rat · Tuesday, December 7, 2027 17:38 – Wednesday, January 5, 2028 (KST)

The Rat month is the deepest point of winter’s Water: the solstice falls inside it, the nights are longest, and everything that matters happens indoors. It rewards planning, study and honest accounting, and punishes big launches. Whatever you seed now shows in spring. Yang Water on top makes the month expansive and unpredictable — ideas travel far and plans change.

Saju counts months from one solar term to the next, so this month begins at 17:38 on Dec 7 and ends on Jan 5. Each sign below is read two ways: how the Rat branch meets the sign’s own branch, and how the month’s Water meets the sign’s element. The best and care days are the days in the month whose day pillar harmonizes or clashes with the sign, computed from the sixty-day cycle.

For the ten Day Masters

Your animal sign is one character of eight. The stem of the month, Yang Water, meets each Day Master as one of the Ten Gods, and that relationship sets the month’s theme more precisely than the animal does. Don’t know your Day Master? Your birth date is enough.

  • Yang Wood (The Tall Pine) — Indirect Resource: deep, unusual insight — study and research pull you in; don’t postpone the one decision that matters.
  • Yin Wood (The Winding Vine) — Direct Resource: support and green lights — approvals, mentors and documents; ask for help and it tends to arrive.
  • Yang Fire (The Midday Sun) — Seven Killings: pressure and proving — demands land on you; meet them squarely and protect your rest.
  • Yin Fire (The Candle Flame) — Direct Officer: structure favors you — applications, reviews and formal steps go well if you follow the rules.
  • Yang Earth (The Great Mountain) — Indirect Wealth: opportunity and spending both swell — decide the budget before the month decides it for you.
  • Yin Earth (The Fertile Field) — Direct Wealth: steady gains — routine work pays, promises hold, and it is a fair month for sensible signatures.
  • Yang Metal (The Raw Blade) — Eating God: a month of ease and output — make, cook, write, show, and enjoy without overdoing it.
  • Yin Metal (The Polished Gem) — Hurting Officer: a month of sharp expression — good for pitching and persuading, careful around authority.
  • Yang Water (The Open Sea) — Friend: a month of standing on your own — peers gather and independence works; keep money matters separate.
  • Yin Water (The Morning Dew) — Rob Wealth: a competitive month — drive rises and so does shared spending; avoid lending.

FAQ

When does the Rat month of 2027 begin and end?

It runs from Tuesday, December 7, 2027 at 17:38 Korea time, the solar term that opens it, to Wednesday, January 5, 2028; the next month begins on Jan 6 at 04:55. Saju months follow the solar terms, not the calendar, which is why the Rat month straddles December and January.

Which zodiac signs have the best December 2027?

the Ox, the Dragon, the Monkey and the Pig — their branches form a harmony with the Rat. The Rabbit, the Horse, the Goat and the Rooster meet more friction and should take the month steadily. The rest have no fixed link and read the month by its element, Water.

What is the month pillar for December 2027?

壬子 — Ren Zi, Yang Water over the Rat. The stem, Yang Water, sets the month’s tone; the branch, the Rat, decides how each sign meets it. It sits inside the 丁未 year.

Is a monthly horoscope by zodiac sign the same as a saju reading?

No. Your animal sign is one of eight characters in a saju chart, so this page reads one character against the month. A personal reading starts from your Day Master, the stem of your birth day; cast your chart and today’s reading compares each day with all of it.
