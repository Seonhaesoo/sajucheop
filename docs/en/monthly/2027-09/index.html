September 2027
己酉
Yin Earth over the Rooster · Wednesday, September 8, 2027 05:28 – Thursday, October 7, 2027 (KST)

The Rooster month is the sharpest Metal of the year, with the autumn equinox inside it. Judgment is clear, standards are high and words can cut. It rewards precision, reviews and finishing touches, and punishes loose promises. Yin Earth on top makes the month practical and nurturing — details, maintenance, and looking after people.

Saju counts months from one solar term to the next, so this month begins at 05:28 on Sep 8 and ends on Oct 7. Each sign below is read two ways: how the Rooster branch meets the sign’s own branch, and how the month’s Earth meets the sign’s element. The best and care days are the days in the month whose day pillar harmonizes or clashes with the sign, computed from the sixty-day cycle.

For the ten Day Masters

Your animal sign is one character of eight. The stem of the month, Yin Earth, meets each Day Master as one of the Ten Gods, and that relationship sets the month’s theme more precisely than the animal does. Don’t know your Day Master? Your birth date is enough.

  • Yang Wood (The Tall Pine) — Direct Wealth: steady gains — routine work pays, promises hold, and it is a fair month for sensible signatures.
  • Yin Wood (The Winding Vine) — Indirect Wealth: opportunity and spending both swell — decide the budget before the month decides it for you.
  • Yang Fire (The Midday Sun) — Hurting Officer: a month of sharp expression — good for pitching and persuading, careful around authority.
  • Yin Fire (The Candle Flame) — Eating God: a month of ease and output — make, cook, write, show, and enjoy without overdoing it.
  • Yang Earth (The Great Mountain) — Rob Wealth: a competitive month — drive rises and so does shared spending; avoid lending.
  • Yin Earth (The Fertile Field) — Friend: a month of standing on your own — peers gather and independence works; keep money matters separate.
  • Yang Metal (The Raw Blade) — Direct Resource: support and green lights — approvals, mentors and documents; ask for help and it tends to arrive.
  • Yin Metal (The Polished Gem) — Indirect Resource: deep, unusual insight — study and research pull you in; don’t postpone the one decision that matters.
  • Yang Water (The Open Sea) — Direct Officer: structure favors you — applications, reviews and formal steps go well if you follow the rules.
  • Yin Water (The Morning Dew) — Seven Killings: pressure and proving — demands land on you; meet them squarely and protect your rest.

FAQ

When does the Rooster month of 2027 begin and end?

It runs from Wednesday, September 8, 2027 at 05:28 Korea time, the solar term that opens it, to Thursday, October 7, 2027; the next month begins on Oct 8 at 21:17. Saju months follow the solar terms, not the calendar, which is why the Rooster month straddles September and October.

Which zodiac signs have the best September 2027?

the Ox, the Dragon, the Snake, the Monkey and the Dog — their branches form a harmony with the Rooster. The Rat, the Tiger, the Rabbit and the Rooster meet more friction and should take the month steadily. The rest have no fixed link and read the month by its element, Earth.

What is the month pillar for September 2027?

己酉 — Ji You, Yin Earth over the Rooster. The stem, Yin Earth, sets the month’s tone; the branch, the Rooster, decides how each sign meets it. It sits inside the 丁未 year.

Is a monthly horoscope by zodiac sign the same as a saju reading?

No. Your animal sign is one of eight characters in a saju chart, so this page reads one character against the month. A personal reading starts from your Day Master, the stem of your birth day; cast your chart and today’s reading compares each day with all of it.
