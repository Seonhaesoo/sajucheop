October 2026
戊戌
Yang Earth over the Dog · Thursday, October 8, 2026 15:29 – Friday, November 6, 2026 (KST)

The Dog month is autumn’s Earth, the storehouse of Fire: the harvest goes into the barn. Gathering, consolidating, closing accounts and preparing for winter all suit it. Disputes over what belongs to whom are the month’s characteristic friction. Yang Earth on top steadies the month: decisions stick, big things move slowly, and reliability counts.

Saju counts months from one solar term to the next, so this month begins at 15:29 on Oct 8 and ends on Nov 6. Each sign below is read two ways: how the Dog branch meets the sign’s own branch, and how the month’s Earth meets the sign’s element. The best and care days are the days in the month whose day pillar harmonizes or clashes with the sign, computed from the sixty-day cycle.

For the ten Day Masters

Your animal sign is one character of eight. The stem of the month, Yang Earth, meets each Day Master as one of the Ten Gods, and that relationship sets the month’s theme more precisely than the animal does. Don’t know your Day Master? Your birth date is enough.

  • Yang Wood (The Tall Pine) — Indirect Wealth: opportunity and spending both swell — decide the budget before the month decides it for you.
  • Yin Wood (The Winding Vine) — Direct Wealth: steady gains — routine work pays, promises hold, and it is a fair month for sensible signatures.
  • Yang Fire (The Midday Sun) — Eating God: a month of ease and output — make, cook, write, show, and enjoy without overdoing it.
  • Yin Fire (The Candle Flame) — Hurting Officer: a month of sharp expression — good for pitching and persuading, careful around authority.
  • Yang Earth (The Great Mountain) — Friend: a month of standing on your own — peers gather and independence works; keep money matters separate.
  • Yin Earth (The Fertile Field) — Rob Wealth: a competitive month — drive rises and so does shared spending; avoid lending.
  • Yang Metal (The Raw Blade) — Indirect Resource: deep, unusual insight — study and research pull you in; don’t postpone the one decision that matters.
  • Yin Metal (The Polished Gem) — Direct Resource: support and green lights — approvals, mentors and documents; ask for help and it tends to arrive.
  • Yang Water (The Open Sea) — Seven Killings: pressure and proving — demands land on you; meet them squarely and protect your rest.
  • Yin Water (The Morning Dew) — Direct Officer: structure favors you — applications, reviews and formal steps go well if you follow the rules.

FAQ

When does the Dog month of 2026 begin and end?

It runs from Thursday, October 8, 2026 at 15:29 Korea time, the solar term that opens it, to Friday, November 6, 2026; the next month begins on Nov 7 at 18:52. Saju months follow the solar terms, not the calendar, which is why the Dog month straddles October and November.

Which zodiac signs have the best October 2026?

the Tiger, the Rabbit, the Horse, the Monkey and the Rooster — their branches form a harmony with the Dog. The Ox, the Dragon, the Snake and the Goat meet more friction and should take the month steadily. The rest have no fixed link and read the month by its element, Earth.

What is the month pillar for October 2026?

戊戌 — Wu Xu, Yang Earth over the Dog. The stem, Yang Earth, sets the month’s tone; the branch, the Dog, decides how each sign meets it. It sits inside the 丙午 year.

Is a monthly horoscope by zodiac sign the same as a saju reading?

No. Your animal sign is one of eight characters in a saju chart, so this page reads one character against the month. A personal reading starts from your Day Master, the stem of your birth day; cast your chart and today’s reading compares each day with all of it.
