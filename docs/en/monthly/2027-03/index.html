March 2027
癸卯
Yin Water over the Rabbit · Saturday, March 6, 2027 04:40 – Sunday, April 4, 2027 (KST)

The Rabbit month is spring at full strength — the gentlest Wood, growth without force. Things that were started now settle in and spread: relationships, projects, habits. A good month for the patient and a frustrating one for anyone who wants results by Friday. Yin Water on top makes the month quiet and intuitive — research, reflection and gentle persistence.

Saju counts months from one solar term to the next, so this month begins at 04:40 on Mar 6 and ends on Apr 4. Each sign below is read two ways: how the Rabbit branch meets the sign’s own branch, and how the month’s Water meets the sign’s element. The best and care days are the days in the month whose day pillar harmonizes or clashes with the sign, computed from the sixty-day cycle.

For the ten Day Masters

Your animal sign is one character of eight. The stem of the month, Yin Water, meets each Day Master as one of the Ten Gods, and that relationship sets the month’s theme more precisely than the animal does. Don’t know your Day Master? Your birth date is enough.

  • Yang Wood (The Tall Pine) — Direct Resource: support and green lights — approvals, mentors and documents; ask for help and it tends to arrive.
  • Yin Wood (The Winding Vine) — Indirect Resource: deep, unusual insight — study and research pull you in; don’t postpone the one decision that matters.
  • Yang Fire (The Midday Sun) — Direct Officer: structure favors you — applications, reviews and formal steps go well if you follow the rules.
  • Yin Fire (The Candle Flame) — Seven Killings: pressure and proving — demands land on you; meet them squarely and protect your rest.
  • Yang Earth (The Great Mountain) — Direct Wealth: steady gains — routine work pays, promises hold, and it is a fair month for sensible signatures.
  • Yin Earth (The Fertile Field) — Indirect Wealth: opportunity and spending both swell — decide the budget before the month decides it for you.
  • Yang Metal (The Raw Blade) — Hurting Officer: a month of sharp expression — good for pitching and persuading, careful around authority.
  • Yin Metal (The Polished Gem) — Eating God: a month of ease and output — make, cook, write, show, and enjoy without overdoing it.
  • Yang Water (The Open Sea) — Rob Wealth: a competitive month — drive rises and so does shared spending; avoid lending.
  • Yin Water (The Morning Dew) — Friend: a month of standing on your own — peers gather and independence works; keep money matters separate.

FAQ

When does the Rabbit month of 2027 begin and end?

It runs from Saturday, March 6, 2027 at 04:40 Korea time, the solar term that opens it, to Sunday, April 4, 2027; the next month begins on Apr 5 at 09:17. Saju months follow the solar terms, not the calendar, which is why the Rabbit month straddles March and April.

Which zodiac signs have the best March 2027?

the Tiger, the Dragon, the Goat, the Dog and the Pig — their branches form a harmony with the Rabbit. The Rat, the Horse, the Monkey and the Rooster meet more friction and should take the month steadily. The rest have no fixed link and read the month by its element, Water.

What is the month pillar for March 2027?

癸卯 — Gui Mao, Yin Water over the Rabbit. The stem, Yin Water, sets the month’s tone; the branch, the Rabbit, decides how each sign meets it. It sits inside the 丁未 year.

Is a monthly horoscope by zodiac sign the same as a saju reading?

No. Your animal sign is one of eight characters in a saju chart, so this page reads one character against the month. A personal reading starts from your Day Master, the stem of your birth day; cast your chart and today’s reading compares each day with all of it.
