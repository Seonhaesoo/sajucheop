How is a monthly horoscope by sign worked out?

Each solar month carries a branch (one of the twelve animals) and a stem (one of five elements). The branch is compared with the Pig’s branch 亥 for harmony or clash, and the stem’s element with the Pig’s own Water. Best and care days come from the day pillars in the same way.
