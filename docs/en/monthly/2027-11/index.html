November 2027
辛亥
Yin Metal over the Pig · Monday, November 8, 2027 00:39 – Monday, December 6, 2027 (KST)

The Pig month opens winter: Water rises, days shorten and the year turns inward. Planning, research and long conversations do well; visible launches less so. It is the month Koreans make kimchi — preparation for a season you cannot see yet. Yin Metal on top brings polish and precision — good for finishing, judging and refining.

Saju counts months from one solar term to the next, so this month begins at 00:39 on Nov 8 and ends on Dec 6. Each sign below is read two ways: how the Pig branch meets the sign’s own branch, and how the month’s Metal meets the sign’s element. The best and care days are the days in the month whose day pillar harmonizes or clashes with the sign, computed from the sixty-day cycle.

For the ten Day Masters

Your animal sign is one character of eight. The stem of the month, Yin Metal, meets each Day Master as one of the Ten Gods, and that relationship sets the month’s theme more precisely than the animal does. Don’t know your Day Master? Your birth date is enough.

  • Yang Wood (The Tall Pine) — Direct Officer: structure favors you — applications, reviews and formal steps go well if you follow the rules.
  • Yin Wood (The Winding Vine) — Seven Killings: pressure and proving — demands land on you; meet them squarely and protect your rest.
  • Yang Fire (The Midday Sun) — Direct Wealth: steady gains — routine work pays, promises hold, and it is a fair month for sensible signatures.
  • Yin Fire (The Candle Flame) — Indirect Wealth: opportunity and spending both swell — decide the budget before the month decides it for you.
  • Yang Earth (The Great Mountain) — Hurting Officer: a month of sharp expression — good for pitching and persuading, careful around authority.
  • Yin Earth (The Fertile Field) — Eating God: a month of ease and output — make, cook, write, show, and enjoy without overdoing it.
  • Yang Metal (The Raw Blade) — Rob Wealth: a competitive month — drive rises and so does shared spending; avoid lending.
  • Yin Metal (The Polished Gem) — Friend: a month of standing on your own — peers gather and independence works; keep money matters separate.
  • Yang Water (The Open Sea) — Direct Resource: support and green lights — approvals, mentors and documents; ask for help and it tends to arrive.
  • Yin Water (The Morning Dew) — Indirect Resource: deep, unusual insight — study and research pull you in; don’t postpone the one decision that matters.

FAQ

When does the Pig month of 2027 begin and end?

It runs from Monday, November 8, 2027 at 00:39 Korea time, the solar term that opens it, to Monday, December 6, 2027; the next month begins on Dec 7 at 17:38. Saju months follow the solar terms, not the calendar, which is why the Pig month straddles November and December.

Which zodiac signs have the best November 2027?

the Rat, the Ox, the Tiger, the Rabbit and the Goat — their branches form a harmony with the Pig. The Dragon, the Snake, the Monkey and the Pig meet more friction and should take the month steadily. The rest have no fixed link and read the month by its element, Metal.

What is the month pillar for November 2027?

辛亥 — Xin Hai, Yin Metal over the Pig. The stem, Yin Metal, sets the month’s tone; the branch, the Pig, decides how each sign meets it. It sits inside the 丁未 year.

Is a monthly horoscope by zodiac sign the same as a saju reading?

No. Your animal sign is one of eight characters in a saju chart, so this page reads one character against the month. A personal reading starts from your Day Master, the stem of your birth day; cast your chart and today’s reading compares each day with all of it.
