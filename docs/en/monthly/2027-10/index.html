October 2027
庚戌
Yang Metal over the Dog · Friday, October 8, 2027 21:17 – Sunday, November 7, 2027 (KST)

The Dog month is autumn’s Earth, the storehouse of Fire: the harvest goes into the barn. Gathering, consolidating, closing accounts and preparing for winter all suit it. Disputes over what belongs to whom are the month’s characteristic friction. Yang Metal on top sharpens everything: cuts, decisions, rules, and a tendency toward bluntness.

Saju counts months from one solar term to the next, so this month begins at 21:17 on Oct 8 and ends on Nov 7. Each sign below is read two ways: how the Dog branch meets the sign’s own branch, and how the month’s Metal meets the sign’s element. The best and care days are the days in the month whose day pillar harmonizes or clashes with the sign, computed from the sixty-day cycle.

For the ten Day Masters

Your animal sign is one character of eight. The stem of the month, Yang Metal, meets each Day Master as one of the Ten Gods, and that relationship sets the month’s theme more precisely than the animal does. Don’t know your Day Master? Your birth date is enough.

  • Yang Wood (The Tall Pine) — Seven Killings: pressure and proving — demands land on you; meet them squarely and protect your rest.
  • Yin Wood (The Winding Vine) — Direct Officer: structure favors you — applications, reviews and formal steps go well if you follow the rules.
  • Yang Fire (The Midday Sun) — Indirect Wealth: opportunity and spending both swell — decide the budget before the month decides it for you.
  • Yin Fire (The Candle Flame) — Direct Wealth: steady gains — routine work pays, promises hold, and it is a fair month for sensible signatures.
  • Yang Earth (The Great Mountain) — Eating God: a month of ease and output — make, cook, write, show, and enjoy without overdoing it.
  • Yin Earth (The Fertile Field) — Hurting Officer: a month of sharp expression — good for pitching and persuading, careful around authority.
  • Yang Metal (The Raw Blade) — Friend: a month of standing on your own — peers gather and independence works; keep money matters separate.
  • Yin Metal (The Polished Gem) — Rob Wealth: a competitive month — drive rises and so does shared spending; avoid lending.
  • Yang Water (The Open Sea) — Indirect Resource: deep, unusual insight — study and research pull you in; don’t postpone the one decision that matters.
  • Yin Water (The Morning Dew) — Direct Resource: support and green lights — approvals, mentors and documents; ask for help and it tends to arrive.

FAQ

When does the Dog month of 2027 begin and end?

It runs from Friday, October 8, 2027 at 21:17 Korea time, the solar term that opens it, to Sunday, November 7, 2027; the next month begins on Nov 8 at 00:39. Saju months follow the solar terms, not the calendar, which is why the Dog month straddles October and November.

Which zodiac signs have the best October 2027?

the Tiger, the Rabbit, the Horse, the Monkey and the Rooster — their branches form a harmony with the Dog. The Ox, the Dragon, the Snake and the Goat meet more friction and should take the month steadily. The rest have no fixed link and read the month by its element, Metal.

What is the month pillar for October 2027?

庚戌 — Geng Xu, Yang Metal over the Dog. The stem, Yang Metal, sets the month’s tone; the branch, the Dog, decides how each sign meets it. It sits inside the 丁未 year.

Is a monthly horoscope by zodiac sign the same as a saju reading?

No. Your animal sign is one of eight characters in a saju chart, so this page reads one character against the month. A personal reading starts from your Day Master, the stem of your birth day; cast your chart and today’s reading compares each day with all of it.
