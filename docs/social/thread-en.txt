3 questions never to ask a Saju reader

1. "When will I die?" — Saju cannot see lifespan. Anyone who says they can, walk away.
2. "Lottery numbers?" — Wealth luck is a current, not digits.
3. "Is my chart bad?" — No bad charts. Only lopsided ones.
